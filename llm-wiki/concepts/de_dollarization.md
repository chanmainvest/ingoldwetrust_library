# De-dollarization

The gradual, structural erosion of the US dollar's role as the world's reserve
and trade-settlement currency — and gold's re-emergence as the neutral reserve
asset of a multipolar order. This is one of the longest-running and most
developed threads in the *In Gold We Trust* corpus, spanning 2014–2026.

> The unipolar, US-dollar-centered monetary order established at Bretton Woods
> (1944) and reinforced by the petrodollar (1974) is in a long, structural,
> tectonic-plate-style unwind. The recurring claim: this is not sudden collapse
> but a protracted **remonetization of gold**.

See also: [central_bank_gold](central_bank_gold.md), [gold_as_money](gold_as_money.md),
[monetary_policy_inflation](monetary_policy_inflation.md).

## The core thesis

The dollar standard ends not because foreigners reject it, but because the
**Triffin Dilemma** is mathematically unsustainable: a national currency used as
the world reserve forces the issuer into permanent deficits and debt
accumulation, until the "risk-free asset ceases to be risk-free" (BIS, 2017).
The recurring solution, across all chapters, is a neutral reserve asset — gold
(commodities, or a synthetic unit like the SDR/bancor). By 2026, the framing
inverts: it is the **US itself** that is ending the dollar standard — via
sanctions, tariffs, and proposed user-fees on foreign Treasury holdings (Miran)
— making gold "the only major asset that can fill the role" because there is no
better alternative.

> "TINA turns into TIAA — There Is An Alternative, and the alternative is gold."
> (2024)

## How the argument evolved

- **2011 — The pre-framework appearance.** Two years before the systematic
  treatment, the *Adieu, Exorbitant Privilege* chapter announces that "more than
  six decades of global dominance by the greenback seem to be coming to an end,"
  and the companion chapter reframes US Treasuries from "the risk-free fixed-
  income paper" into "the risky no-income paper" on the S&P outlook downgrade.
  Both already cite gold as the beneficiary of reserve-currency doubt. The theme
  is present but not yet operationalized.
- **2014** — A side theme. Russia sells Treasuries; Gazprom Neft switches to
  euros; Putin calls the dollar "parasitic." Framed as gradual, multi-decade.
- **2017** — First dedicated chapter; the systematic framework. Introduces the
  reserve-currency-mortality chart (Portugal→Spain→Netherlands→France→UK→USA),
  the petrodollar mechanics, the ECB's mark-to-market gold model vs. the Fed's
  frozen $42.22/oz, and Shadow Gold Price math (M0 ~$13,749/oz, M2 ~$50,746/oz).
  The Shanghai Gold Exchange (Sept 2014) is called a "reopening of the gold
  window" (Gromen).
- **2018** — Petroyuan and "Zugzwang." Shanghai crude-oil yuan futures launch
  (March 2018). Gromen's thesis that every dollar policy move worsens the US
  position. Conclusion: "no singular reserve currency… a multipolar trade
  system."
- **2019** — Europe joins; the Freegold thesis enters. Juncker: 80% of EU
  energy imports are priced in USD — "absurd." INSTEX created to bypass the
  dollar for Iran trade. Central banks bought 651 t gold in 2018 (+74% YoY).
  FOFOA's USD 55K gold target.
- **2020** — "The endgame has begun." Carney's Jackson Hole call for a
  "synthetic world currency"; the full de-dollarization timeline (de Gaulle
  1965 → Carney 2019). Gromen's $20,000/oz scenario if the Fed "writes up gold."
- **2021** — Saudi Aramco's 50-year supply pledge to China; USD reserve share
  falls to 59% (lowest since 1995). Stephen Roach's 35% dollar-correction
  thesis. Russia's reserve mix: 30% euros, 22% gold, 22% USD.
- **2022** — The inflection: freezing ~$300 bn of Russian FX reserves is framed
  as the biggest monetary rupture since Nixon 1971 — "the West closed the FX
  window." Zoltan Pozsar's **Bretton Woods III** thesis: a commodity-backed
  Eastern monetary order. Putin's gas-for-rubles + fixed ruble-gold rate.
- **2023** — "The Final Showdown." The five-phase framework is formalized.
  Stephen Jen: USD lost ~11% reserve share since 2016, falling "at ten times the
  average pace." Record central-bank gold buying of 1,136 t (2022). Xi's Riyadh
  speech = "dawn of the Petroyuan."
- **2024** — "Enter the Dragon." A notably balanced chapter emphasizing dollar
  *strength* (Brent Johnson's Dollar Milkshake, network effects: USD in 88% of
  FX transactions, 47% of SWIFT). But the counter-trend accelerates: RMB hit
  4.6% of SWIFT; Russia-China trade >90% in national currencies. "Is gold eating
  USTs?" — foreign central banks stopped net-buying Treasuries in Q3 2021.
- **2026** — The conceptual climax. The framing inverts: the **US is ending the
  standard**. Miran's user-fee on foreign Treasury holdings; stablecoins (GENIUS
  Act) as infrastructure to separate the dollar's transactional role from its
  reserve role. Gold overtakes the euro as the #2 reserve asset. But gold is
  declared "not the ideal" final architecture — the likely endpoint is a
  negotiated Bancor-like system, "after a painful period of disorder."

## Key mechanisms

1. **Petrodollar erosion / petroyuan rise.** Oil invoicing currency determines
   reserve demand; lose oil pricing, lose the recycling loop that funds US
   deficits. Tracked 2014→2024 (Gazprom→SGE→Shanghai crude-yuan futures→Saudi
   Aramco pledge→Xi's Riyadh speech→first India-UAE rupee oil trade). The full
   gold/oil-ratio and petrodollar-recycling mechanics are dissected in
   [petrodollar & the oil-gold nexus](petrodollar_oil_gold.md).
2. **Sanctions weaponization / reserve freezing.** The 2022 freeze of Russian
   reserves redefined "risk-free." Yellen herself conceded sanctions "could
   undermine the hegemony of the dollar" (2023).
3. **ECB mark-to-market gold model vs. the Fed's frozen $42.22/oz.** The
   Eurosystem values its 10,800+ t quarterly; the US books gold at the 1973
   statutory price. Presented as the silent blueprint for a floating-gold
   reference system (2017, 2019, 2022).
4. **Central-bank gold accumulation as the empirical signal.** From 651 t
   (2018) to a record 1,136 t (2022) to gold overtaking the euro as the #2
   reserve (2026). See [central_bank_gold](central_bank_gold.md).
5. **Shadow Gold Price / revaluation arithmetic.** If gold re-monetizes, the
   implied price is orders of magnitude higher: US M2 backing requires ~$79,699/oz;
   China M2 ~$579,621/oz (2024). Gromen's $20,000/oz "if the Fed writes up
   gold" (2020).
6. **CBDCs and alternative payment rails.** m-CBDC Bridge / Project mBridge,
   CIPS as a SWIFT alternative — letting states settle bilateral trade without
   dollars (2020–2024).
7. **The yuan walk-back (the most significant internal correction).** 2018–2023
   built up the petroyuan/yuan-reserve thesis; 2026 concedes the RMB has *failed*
   as a reserve alternative — its share fell from a 2.84% peak (Q1 2022) to
   2.1%, and "a reserve currency that cannot be freely acquired or repatriated
   is not a serious reserve currency."
8. **The Triffin Dilemma as the theoretical spine.** By 2026 elevated to the
   central organizing concept: the US is the "residual adjuster" of global
   excess savings; reserve status is reclassified from "privilege" to "burden."

## Nuance

- **"Tectonic plates, not avalanche."** Every chapter from 2019 onward explicitly
  disclaims imminent dollar collapse. The 2024 chapter leans hardest into dollar
  *strength* almost as a corrective.
- **Gold is declared "not the ideal" final architecture (2026).** A striking
  nuance after a decade of gold-bullish framing — a reserve asset that becomes
  more volatile precisely when reserves are most needed is unlikely to be the
  final answer. The realistic endpoint is a negotiated Bancor-like system.
- **Internal tension between the "Eastern gold standard" narrative and the "US
  policy drives it" narrative.** Pre-2022 attributes the shift to Eastern
  accumulation; 2026 relocates the driver to US policy choices. Not fully
  reconciled.
