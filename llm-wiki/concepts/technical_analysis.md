# Technical analysis & price targets

The recurring technical-analysis apparatus — the 1970s-bull-market comparison as
analytical anchor, the "Quo vadis, aurum?" price-target chapters, seasonality,
and the proprietary Incrementum Active Aurum Signal.

> "Technical analysis is certainly not an exact science, but rather a useful tool
> for determining the location and timing of investments." (2019)

See also: [portfolio_diversification](portfolio_diversification.md),
[gold_mining_stocks](gold_mining_stocks.md), [silver](silver.md).

## The core approach

The technical chapter (written by Ronald-Peter Stöferle, CMT) combines classical
chart analysis with contrarian sentiment reading, as a complement to the
fundamental/macro work. The recurring toolkit:

- **The 1970s-bull-market comparison** — the single most persistent framing
  device, present from 2009 to 2026, used to argue the current cycle is
  structurally healthier (stepwise vs. speculative parabola) and to project the
  endgame shape.
- **"Quo vadis, aurum?"** — a dedicated closing chapter setting multi-year price
  targets grounded in a proprietary gold-coverage-ratio-of-the-monetary-base model.
- **Sentiment (contrarian)** — the SentimenTrader Optix index (house favorite from
  2016), Bloomberg analyst consensus (treated contrarian), the Hulbert index.
- **Coppock curve** — the house momentum indicator (on a buy signal continuously
  since end-2015).
- **The Midas Touch Gold Model™** (Florian Grummes) — a multi-component
  buy/sell-signal table (gold in USD/INR/CNY, ratios, real rates, DXY).
- **CoT report, seasonality, ratio analysis.**

## How the analysis evolved

- **2009-2010 (Erste Group)** — The "current vs. last great bull market"
  comparison: the 1970s rally was a speculative blow-off (+2,000% in 10 years);
  the 2000s rally is a "lasting paradigm shift" — stepwise, "we have not seen the
  blow-off top yet."
- **2013** — Pivot to contrarian/sentiment after the 2011 peak crash. CoT in depth
  (commercials reduced net shorts 84%); Hulbert allocation at -44% (all-time low).
  Explicitly invokes the "1974-1976 mid-cycle correction" analog.
- **2016** — Adds the Bloomberg-consensus-as-contrarian method; the Midas Touch
  Gold Model™.
- **2019** — Formalizes the Coppock curve, KST, MACD; the inverse
  shoulder-head-shoulder on the 200-day MA (target ~$1,800). First Dow-theory
  three-phases framing.
- **2022** — Adds the cup-and-handle formation (target ~$2,700) on the still-active
  Coppock buy.
- **2024** — The pivot: cup-and-handle "finally" resolves upward. Adds the
  proprietary **Incrementum Active Aurum Signal** and a dedicated Calendar
  Anomalies chapter. CoT formally retired ("prices are now being set in Asia").
- **2026** — Peak elaboration. The 1976-1980 acceleration analog dominates
  ("likely to accelerate further, much as it did 1976-1980"). Gold ATH $5,602
  (Jan 29, 2026). The Quo Vadis chapter introduces shadow-gold-price / monetary-
  backing framework.

## Key recurring methods

1. **The 1970s comparison.** 2009, 2010, 2013 (the 1974-76 mid-cycle analog),
   2024, and most explicitly 2026 ("similar to 1976-1980"). The central claim:
   today's bull is healthier than 1980's blow-off.
2. **The Coppock curve.** House momentum indicator from 2019; on a buy signal
   continuously since end-2015 across all four years.
3. **The Midas Touch Gold Model™ (Grummes).** Present 2016-2026; synthesizes
   signals across currencies, ratios, and real rates.
4. **SentimenTrader Optix.** Thresholds: >75 = caution, ≤30 = pessimism. The 2026
   Optix hit 20 at end-2022 — "which coincided exactly with the low in the gold
   price" — and gold has "nearly tripled" since.
5. **"Quo vadis, aurum?" price model.** Base-case 2030 target: **$4,800** (stated
   2020, reaffirmed 2024, reached five years early in 2026). Inflationary 2030
   scenario: **$8,900**.
6. **Calendar anomalies / seasonality.** Evolved from monthly-average charts
   (2009) → Seasonax political-cycle sub-charts → the full 2024 quantitative
   backtest (Halloween effect on GDX: winter +8.99% vs. summer -4.00%).
7. **The Incrementum Active Aurum Signal (2024).** A mining-stock timing signal
   (cycle + fundamental components). Backtest: average 1.5 signal changes/year
   since 1971; cumulative performance "over 7,000%" vs. passive buy-and-hold.
   All four Incrementum house models (Inflation Signal, SEGPM, Active Aurum,
   Midas Touch) are consolidated in
   [proprietary models](proprietary_models.md).

## Notable data & price targets

- **2020 → 2024 → 2026:** the decade target of **$4,800 by 2030** (stated 2020)
  was reached in 2026 — "five years ahead of schedule." Upgraded to an
  "inflationary scenario" of **$8,900 by end-decade** (2026).
- **2024:** cup-and-handle resolved above $2,075; first inverted SHS target ~$2,535
  ("missed by just under $100"). Comparison rates: 2000s >14%/yr, 1970s ~27%/yr.
- **2026:** Gold ATH **$5,602** (Jan 29, 2026); subsequent 21.4% crash to ~$4,400.
  Realized CAGR since the "golden decade" start: 19.7%. Shadow gold price: 25% M2
  backing = $60,000+. Silver 2025: +146.8% (strongest year since 1979).
- **Analyst consensus median for 2030 (2026):** $3,405; forward curve $5,556. The
  authors frame this divergence as reason to distrust the sell side.

## Nuance — accuracy of price targets

The reports show a strong track record on directional/long-term calls but are
notably honest about timing/short-term misses:

- **Where right:** the 2020 decade-target reached in 2026; the Coppock buy from
  end-2015 caught the secular bottom; the 2024 cup-and-handle breakout executed
  precisely. 2026 self-assessment: "we were too conservative."
- **Where they acknowledge misses:** 2016 (the 2015 "final sell-off" never
  materialized); 2019 (the "wash-out" went further than expected); 2022 (the
  cup-handle resolution was "much tougher and more protracted than expected");
  2024 (the first target "missed by just under $100").
- **Structural permabull bias.** Every annual conclusion since 2016 recommends
  accumulating on weakness; the "bear" content is always tactical, never
  strategic. A subtle revision tendency: when the 2020 target was hit early, the
  2026 report upgraded to $8,900 rather than declaring victory.
- **2026 publishes a "bear case for gold"** in the Quo Vadis chapter (restoration
  of Fed credibility, CB-demand decline, geopolitical easing, liquidity paradox) —
  unusual intellectual honesty for a gold-bull house.
