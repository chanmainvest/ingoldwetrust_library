# Gold/Silver Ratio Movement

The Gold/Silver Ratio (GSR) is a critical metric for assessing the relative valuation and potential direction of both precious metals markets. Analyzing its movement helps investors gauge shifts in sentiment between gold and silver, often signaling potential entry or exit points based on established technical patterns.

## How the argument evolved

The analysis of the Gold/Silver Ratio evolved across the two reports, providing contrasting signals regarding its recent trajectory. In the **2020 — 23 Technical Analysis**, the focus was on the ratio's peak performance. This chapter indicated that the GSR had reached a new high, specifically noting that "gold/silver ratio (GSR) had reached a new all-time high in mid-March at 1:125." This peak was interpreted as a bullish indicator for the lower metal, as the report concluded that "Since April 20th the GSR has provided a clear buy signal for silver."

In contrast, the **2026 — 26 Technical Analysis** presented a view suggesting a potential reversal or counter-move. This chapter described a shift in the ratio's structure, noting that the gold/silver ratio "moved to a lower boundary of a downtrend channel, suggesting a potential counter-move." Specifically, the analysis pinpointed a technical level where "The gold/silver ratio turned precisely at 43.57 on the lower boundary of the major downtrend channel." This technical observation led to the conclusion that "a prolonged consolidation or countertrend move could theoretically push the ratio back toward 82." Thus, the narrative moved from identifying a recent high and subsequent buying opportunity to identifying a technical low that could initiate a move back toward a higher valuation.
