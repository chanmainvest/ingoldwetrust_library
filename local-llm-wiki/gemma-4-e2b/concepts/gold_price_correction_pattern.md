# Gold Price Correction Pattern

The concept of the Gold Price Correction Pattern examines specific technical chart formations that suggest a temporary reversal or consolidation in the prevailing gold price trend. This analysis is significant as it seeks to identify potential turning points within the market structure, suggesting that sharp price movements may be subject to predictable, corrective phases.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of the Gold Price Correction Pattern evolved across the analyzed reports, focusing on the formation and resolution of specific chart patterns following significant price movements. In **2018**, the analysis centered on a specific price trajectory. The chart analysis revealed a correction from USD 280 to USD 1,920, which was identified as forming an "inverse shoulder-head-shoulder pattern." The report explicitly stated that "the impulsive increase of USD 280 to USD 1,920 has been subject to correction since 2011." Furthermore, this pattern was interpreted as evidence of a potential upward resolution, concluding that "This correction has led to the establishment of an impressive inverse shoulder-head-shoulder formation that could be resolved upwards in due course."

The perspective in **2019** reinforced and slightly refined the findings from the previous year by focusing on the same price action. The analysis reiterated the observation of an impulsive rise from USD 280 to USD 1,920, confirming that this movement "has been corrected since 2011." The core technical structure identified was the "inverse shoulder-head-shoulder formation." The conclusion remained bullish regarding the pattern's potential, asserting that "an impressive inverse shoulder-head-shoulder formation has been forming since 2013, which could explosively resolve upwards." Thus, the evolution shows a consistent focus on identifying and interpreting the inverse shoulder-head-shoulder structure as a mechanism for price correction and subsequent upward movement.
