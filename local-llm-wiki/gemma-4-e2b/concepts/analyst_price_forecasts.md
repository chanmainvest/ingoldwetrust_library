# Analyst Price Forecasts

Analyst price forecasts within the 'In Gold We Trust' corpus represent the evolving sentiment and quantitative expectations of market analysts regarding the future price of gold. This concept is significant as it tracks the shift in professional opinion—from skepticism to bullishness—and how those expectations are priced into market projections over time.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of analyst price forecasts in the corpus shows a clear evolution between the two documented years. In **2016**, the sentiment surrounding gold price predictions was notably negative. The analysis indicated that analysts had "lost interest in gold, with average price targets being relatively low." This skepticism was so pronounced that one observation noted, "If one looks at last year's gold price forecasts for the end of 2016 it appears as though analysts had lost their interest and faith in gold completely." Quantitatively, the average price target recorded during this period stood at USD 1,050.

By **2020**, the narrative shifted significantly, reflecting a change in analyst conviction. The analysis demonstrated that the relationship between market movement and forecasting was procyclical. Specifically, the report noted that "Analysts raised price targets in a procyclical manner during the price upswing." This suggests that when gold prices were rising, analysts were moving to higher price expectations, indicating a growing, rather than fading, belief in the asset's future value.
