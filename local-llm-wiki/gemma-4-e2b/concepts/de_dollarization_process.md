# De-Dollarization Process

De-dollarization describes a long-term, evolving process where the global monetary system is reorganizing itself away from the dominance of the US Dollar toward a more balanced, multipolar structure. This shift is significant because it suggests a fundamental restructuring of global economic power, moving toward a system that is more resilient and representative of all major global powers.

## How the argument evolved

The concept of de-dollarization was introduced in the **2018** report, which framed it as a foundational reorganization of the monetary system. The analysis in **2018 — 07 De Dollarization From The Dollar To Gold Via The Yuan And The Euro** defined the process as "the process of 'de-dollarization' represents" a move toward a "new system that is balanced from the perspective of all major powers – and that can serve as the foundation of a multipolar global economic order." This initial view established the theoretical framework for this transition.

The perspective evolved in the subsequent analysis in **2020**. The **2020 — 10 De Dollarization 2020 The Endgame Has Begun** report moved from defining the process to observing its acceleration in the present day. This chapter noted that the analysis has been tracking "the long-running, creeping process of 'de-dollarization.'" The conclusion drawn in this later work was that this trend is not merely theoretical but is manifesting in reality, asserting that "the monetary endgame has begun."
