# Short-term disinflationary dynamics

This concept explores the short-term mechanisms driving disinflationary pressures, focusing specifically on the interplay between monetary policy actions and macroeconomic conditions. It is significant because understanding these dynamics helps investors gauge the immediate impact of central bank tightening and external economic factors on the gold market.

## How the argument evolved

The analysis of short-term disinflationary dynamics began by examining the direct impact of monetary policy tools. In the **2024** report, the focus was placed squarely on quantitative tightening and interest rate adjustments as the primary drivers. The report specifically detailed the relationship between these actions and the market, noting that "Short-term disinflationary dynamics" are intrinsically linked to "Quantitative tightening" and "Interest rate hikes or postponement of the start of the rate cut cycle."

The perspective evolved in the **2025** analysis by broadening the scope to include external macroeconomic pressures that contribute to this disinflationary environment. While the **2024** chapter focused on the policy mechanism itself, the **2025** chapter integrated external economic slowdowns into the narrative. This evolution showed that the short-term dynamics are not solely driven by central bank actions but are also influenced by the broader economic landscape. Specifically, the **2025** report identified that the short-term dynamics include an "Economic slowdown due to trade wars," alongside the direct effects of "Quantitative tightening." This synthesis suggests that the disinflationary environment is a confluence of tightening monetary policy and external trade-related economic deceleration.
