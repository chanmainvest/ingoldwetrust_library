# Technical Analysis Scope

The scope of technical analysis within the 'In Gold We Trust' reports establishes a distinct, objective layer of market observation that complements traditional fundamental and macroeconomic assessments. This approach focuses specifically on the price action and market structure, providing a technical picture independent of underlying economic or political narratives.

## How the argument evolved

The initial articulation of the technical scope in **2016** established a foundational requirement for comprehensive market analysis. The report explicitly stated that "A comprehensive analysis of the gold market definitely has to include an analysis of the technical picture as well." Furthermore, the focus was narrowed to ensure objectivity, emphasizing that "It is important in this context to focus exclusively on technical conditions in terms of price and market structure." This initial view positioned technical analysis as a necessary, standalone component of any thorough gold market assessment.

The perspective evolved in **2019** by integrating this technical view into a broader analytical framework. The analysis moved beyond simply presenting a technical snapshot to positioning it as a complementary tool. In this later iteration, the report stated that "we complement our comprehensive macroeconomic, (geo-)political, and fundamental analysis with a short view on the technical status quo of the gold market." This evolution shows a shift from treating technical analysis as a standalone requirement to integrating it as a short-term status check that supports the longer-term fundamental and macro views.
