# Monetary Policy and Inflation

## Analysis

### 2009 — 01 Introduction
A determined government can generate positive inflation by increasing the money supply under a paper-money system.
Evidence from source:
> under a paper-money system, a determined government can always generate higher spending and hence positive inflation

### 2021 — 04 Global Demographics Turn Inflationary
The text contrasts the effect of quantitative easing on inflation and money supply.
Evidence from source:
> QE alone is antideflationary, or inflationary for asset prices in particular
> It is broad money supply rather than base money supply that has a direct impact on consumer prices

### 2023 — 05 Status Quo Of The Inflation Trend
A restrictive monetary policy is necessary to counteract inflation, and monetary measures have been exhausted.
Evidence from source:
> a restrictive monetary policy would be necessary to counteract the impending wave of inflation at an early stage.
> With the monetary and fiscal Corona measures, all previous limits have been blown.

### 2023 — 08 The Showdown In Monetary Policy

Evidence from source:
