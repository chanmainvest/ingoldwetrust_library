# Commitment of Traders Report

The Commitment of Traders (CoT) report serves as a crucial tool for assessing market sentiment by analyzing the positions held by commercial traders and large speculators. It provides insight into whether the market is currently over-committed or under-committed, thereby signaling potential future volatility or trend direction.

## How the argument evolved

The analysis of the Commitment of Traders report has evolved across different timeframes, providing varying interpretations of market positioning. In the **2011** analysis, the view presented a largely positive assessment of the market structure. Specifically, the report indicated that "The Commitment of Traders Report (CoT) currently paints a largely positive picture," noting that "Neither commercials nor large speculators have taken extreme positions." This suggests a balanced market where neither side is overwhelmingly committed to a single direction.

The perspective shifted in the **2026** analysis, focusing more specifically on the speculative side of the market. This later assessment indicated that the Commitments of Traders report reveals that speculators are positioned at a level below historical highs. The evidence cited in this period showed that "The speculative net long positions of non-commercials stood at around 160,000 contracts – a thoroughly constructive but by no means euphoric level." This suggests that while speculators are positioned constructively, the level of commitment is not yet euphoric.
