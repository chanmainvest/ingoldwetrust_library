# Secular Bull Gold Market

The core thesis of the 'In Gold We Trust' corpus posits that gold is not merely subject to short-term volatility but is fundamentally positioned within a long-term, secular bull market. This perspective suggests that current price action, even during sharp movements, is indicative of a healthy, underlying trend rather than a temporary anomaly.

## How the argument evolved

The concept of the secular bull gold market is established early in the corpus, maintaining a consistent long-term view on the asset. In **2016**, the authors explicitly maintained this foundational belief, stating that "continued to stand by our thesis that gold was still in a secular bull market." This initial framing sets the stage for viewing price action through a long-term lens.

The perspective is reinforced and refined in later analyses, confirming the structural integrity of this market. By **2026**, the analysis confirms that the secular bull gold market is not only intact but that price movements are integrated into a broader, healthy pattern. The report asserts that "The secular bull gold market remains intact." Furthermore, the interpretation of price fluctuations is framed constructively; for instance, the parabolic rally to the all-time high of USD 5,589 and the subsequent sharp correction to USD 4,098 are interpreted as "part of a healthy consolidation pattern." This suggests that volatility is not a sign of a market reversal but rather a necessary component of a sound, long-term upward trajectory.
