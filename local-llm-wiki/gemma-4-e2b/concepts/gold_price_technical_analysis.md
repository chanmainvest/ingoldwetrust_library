# Gold Price Technical Analysis

This concept explores the application of technical indicators to forecast gold price movements, treating market price action as a language that can be deciphered through established charting tools. The significance lies in providing a framework for identifying potential entry and exit points based on historical price patterns, moving beyond purely fundamental analysis to incorporate market structure.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of gold price technical analysis evolved across the two reports, moving from specific, quantifiable levels in **2021** to a more conceptual, pattern-based approach in **2026**. In the **2021** analysis, the focus was on using established technical tools to pinpoint precise price targets. For instance, the report analyzed the gold price using indicators such as Fibonacci retracement and momentum curves. A specific example of this application was the identification of support levels, where "The support level at USD 1,680 marked exactly the 38.2% Fibonacci retracement of the uptrend from 2015 to 2020." Furthermore, the report defined trading signals based on indicator behavior, noting that "A buy signal occurs when the indicator, which is below the zero line, turns upwards, i.e. assumes a positive value."

By **2026**, the perspective shifted from pinpointing specific levels to understanding the market's structural language. The analysis became less about exact numerical levels and more about recognizing overarching market cycles. The **2026** report framed technical analysis as a form of market literacy, stating, "Technical analysis is a language; once you’re fluent, the market speaks to you." This fluency implies that understanding the patterns allows for better navigation of the market. The ultimate goal of this advanced technical view was to confirm the end of a potential correction. The report suggested that "definitive confirmation that the ABC correction has ended will only come once gold has sustainably broken above the high of the potential B wave at USD 5,419."
