# Euro as Alternative Currency

The euro should be perceived as a serious alternative to the US dollar in energy markets by various stakeholders.

### 2023 — 10 De Dollarization The Final Showdown
The euro was considered an alternative currency, chosen by China and Russia for trade settlements.
Evidence from source:
> the euro was chosen by China and Russia in early 2022 as the settlement currency for their huge gas deal.
> the euro is the only currency that has managed to achieve significant reserve status next to the US dollar so far.
