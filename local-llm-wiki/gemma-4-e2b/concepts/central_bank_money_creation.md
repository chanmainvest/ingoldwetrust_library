# Central Bank Money Creation

Central banks will create more irredeemable money globally this year.

## Analysis

### 2015 — 01 Introduction
Central banks will create more irredeemable money globally this year.
Evidence from source:
> central banks will create more irredeemable money this year than ever before

### 2017 — 01 Introduction
Central banks created a large amount of money to purchase financial securities.
Evidence from source:
> the largest central banks created the equivalent of almost USD 1,000 bn. worth of central bank money ex nihilo
> the fresh currency was not used to fund philanthropic projects but to purchase financial securities
