# Gold Price Seasonality

The gold price exhibits pronounced seasonality due to factors like the Indian wedding season and festivals.

## Analysis

### 2008 — 28 Technical Analysis Of The Gold Sector
The gold price exhibits pronounced seasonality due to factors like the Indian wedding season and festivals.
Evidence from source:
> pronounced seasonality of the price
> so-called wedding season as well as the Diwali Festival in India

### 2011 — 24 Technical Analysis Of The Gold Price
Profound seasonality, caused by wedding and Diwali festivals, causes the gold price to perform best in the fourth and first quarters.
Evidence from source:
> An essential reason for the recent sideways phase of the gold price is the profound seasonality
> Due to the strong seasonality we expect the gold price to move sideways until the end of July

### 2012 — 18 Technical Analysis Of The Gold Price
The gold price exhibits seasonality, suggesting it should move sideways until the end of July.
Evidence from source:
> Due to the strong seasonality we believe that the gold price should be moving sideways until the end of July.
> The gold price has increased in September in 65% of the cases

### 2024 — 24 Technical Analysis
June is traditionally a weak month for the gold price, and a seasonal component awaits the gold market until mid-December.
Evidence from source:
> June in particular is traditionally an extremely weak month for the gold price.
> a dark red seasonal component awaits the gold bulls in June.
> the seasonal component is not really well-disposed towards the gold market until mid-December.
