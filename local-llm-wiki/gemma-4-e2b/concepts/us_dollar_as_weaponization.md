# US Dollar as Weaponization

The concept of the US Dollar as a weapon posits that the United States is increasingly leveraging its dominant position in the global financial system to exert geopolitical and economic pressure on other nations. This weaponization involves the deliberate use of the dollar as a political instrument, leading to concerns among other nations regarding its role in international transactions and monetary policy.

## How the argument evolved

The critique of the US dollar as a weapon emerged in the **2019** analysis, where the focus was on the observable behavior of the US. During this period, the report noted that "Many politicians and central bankers complain that the US is using the US dollar as a weapon." This observation was framed by the assertion that "the behavior of the USA is becoming more aggressive, and the use of this “Dollar weapon” can be observed more frequently."

This concern evolved and gained geopolitical weight in subsequent analysis, particularly in **2024**. The later perspective expanded the scope from mere complaint to active application of coercive power. In this later view, the text explicitly notes that the US dollar is utilized "as a political instrument, which has caused concerns among other nations." This weaponization is demonstrated by the United States applying restrictive measures to certain countries, such as "placing restrictions on transactions, freezing assets, etc." The severity of this action is highlighted by external reactions, evidenced by the statement that "Putin slammed the US for weaponizing the US dollar."
