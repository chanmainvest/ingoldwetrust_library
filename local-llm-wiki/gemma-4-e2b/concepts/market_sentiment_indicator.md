# Market Sentiment Indicator

The Market Sentiment Indicator is a framework that utilizes specific sentiment indices, such as the Optimism Index (Optix), to gauge prevailing market mood and signal potential shifts in investor psychology. This indicator is significant because it posits that prevailing sentiment, particularly when it reaches a certain level of consensus, can serve as a contrarian signal, advising caution rather than bullishness.

## How the argument evolved

The concept of using sentiment as a contrarian tool emerged across the analyzed reports, evolving from a simple identification of a sentiment measure to a defined trading rule. In **2016**, the discussion introduced the Optimism Index (Optix) as a sentiment indicator that should be interpreted as a contrary indicator, with the statement, "high optimism readings have to be seen as negative and vice versa."

The focus sharpened in subsequent years as the specific index was named. By **2020**, the analysis centered on the SentimenTrader Optix Index, noting, "One of our favorite sentiment indicators is the SentimenTrader Optix Index." This led to the development of a specific threshold for caution: "If the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it is time to be more cautious."

This cautious interpretation was reinforced in **2021**, where the Optix index was described as tracking market mood, with the observation, "If the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it is time to become more cautious."

The role of the indicator as a signal for pessimism became more explicitly defined in **2022**. Here, the Optix Index was used to gauge market sentiment, with the reasoning that "When public opinion forms a strong consensus, this broad consensus is a good contraindicator." The actionable advice remained consistent: "If the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it is time to become more cautious."

Finally, by **2026**, the concept solidified into a clear, actionable rule. The Optix Index was confirmed to serve as a contrarian indicator, signaling caution when it rises above 75 points, summarized by the directive: "When the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it’s time to be more cautious."
