# Distribution Phase Characteristics

The distribution phase in the context of the 'In Gold We Trust' reports describes a critical market turning point where the dynamics shift from speculative accumulation to informed selling, signaling the end of a speculative mania. This phase is significant because it involves the actions of sophisticated investors, and the accompanying sentiment—characterized by a sense that "this time is different"—is crucial for understanding market structure during price declines.

## How the argument evolved

The concept of the distribution phase was presented across two distinct reports, **2019** and **2020**, both converging on the role of informed investors in driving market dynamics. In the **2019 — 18 Technical Analysis**, the description focused on the psychological and behavioral aspects surrounding this transition. This analysis characterized the distribution phase as being marked by informed investors actively reducing their holdings, accompanied by a heightened level of sentiment. Specifically, the report noted that "During this final mania phase, the group of informed investors who have accumulated from near the low point begins to reduce their positions." Furthermore, the environment during this period was defined by a specific psychological framing, characterized by “this time is different” sentiment.

The **2020 — 23 Technical Analysis** reinforced and expanded upon this view, solidifying the core mechanism of the distribution phase while adding a dimension related to external market commentary. This analysis maintained the focus on informed investors initiating selling, stating that the distribution phase is characterized by "informed investors reducing positions and media/analysts outperforming." The core action remains the same: the sophisticated group is exiting their positions. The **2020** report emphasizes that the distribution is confirmed not just by investor action but also by external validation, as the media and analysts begin to outperform the market. The central theme remains the same across both years: the market structure is defined by the informed participants moving out of the market, signaling a shift from speculative fervor to orderly liquidation.
