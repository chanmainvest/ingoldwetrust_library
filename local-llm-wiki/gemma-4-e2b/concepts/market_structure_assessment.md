# Market structure assessment

The market structure assessment within the 'In Gold We Trust' reports focuses on evaluating the underlying framework of the gold and silver markets, examining technical patterns and prevailing sentiment to determine the market's current health and direction. This analysis is crucial for investors as it seeks to quantify the technical validity of market movements and sentiment indicators.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of market structure assessment in the reports shows a consistent, positive technical outlook across the analyzed periods. In **2018**, the technical analysis specifically addressed the structure of the gold and silver markets, concluding with a clear positive finding. The report explicitly stated, "Our technical assessment of the gold and silver market is positive with regard to market structure, sentiment, and price pattern." This finding established a baseline view where the technical indicators pointed toward a favorable market structure.

This positive technical view was reinforced and confirmed in subsequent analysis. By **2021**, the analysis of market structure, sentiment, and price patterns again resulted in a positive technical assessment. The narrative flow from the previous year to the subsequent analysis suggests a continuous validation of the market's structure. The conclusion drawn from this deeper analysis was that "The analysis of market structure, sentiment, and price patterns leads to a positive technical assessment." This evolution demonstrates a consistent, positive technical conviction regarding the underlying structure of the gold market.
