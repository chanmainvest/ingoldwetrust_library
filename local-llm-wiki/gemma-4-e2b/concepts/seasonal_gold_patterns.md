# Seasonal Gold Patterns

The analysis of seasonal gold patterns within the 'In Gold We Trust' corpus examines the recurring temporal biases that influence gold price movements, suggesting that specific months within the year exhibit predictable, cyclical momentum. This concept is significant as it helps investors identify periods where technical patterns align with seasonal demand or supply dynamics, potentially offering predictive edges beyond purely macroeconomic factors.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of seasonal gold patterns evolved across the analyzed reports, consistently focusing on the interplay between the late spring/early summer and the mid-summer months. In **2020 — 23 Technical Analysis**, the focus established a clear pattern of seasonal tailwinds, noting that "seasonal tailwinds set in from mid-May but are reversed at the beginning of July." This was complemented by the observation that "another seasonally strong phase begins in mid-September."

The perspective shifted in **2022 — 24 Technical Analysis** to explicitly identify the counter-seasonal pressures. This report noted seasonal headwinds, specifically stating, "seasonal headwinds set in from the end of May, but reverse again at the beginning of July." Furthermore, this analysis introduced a specific weakness within the seasonal cycle, noting that "June is, after May, the second weakest month in election years."

The findings from **2023 — 22 Technical Analysis** reinforced the cyclical nature of these seasonal shifts. This report mirrored the previous findings by expecting seasonal headwinds from the end of May, which were expected to reverse at the beginning of July, echoing the pattern observed in the earlier reports.
