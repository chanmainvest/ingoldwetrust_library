# Incrementum Inflation Signal

The Incrementum Inflation Signal is a proprietary analytical tool designed to gauge the actual level of monetary inflation reaching the real economy by synthesizing various market-based indicators. This signal is crucial for investment strategy, as it provides a forward-looking measure of inflationary trends to guide asset allocation decisions.

## How the argument evolved

The concept of the Incrementum Inflation Signal emerged from a need to quantify the relationship between monetary inflation and its impact on the real economy. In **2014**, the initial framework was established where the proprietary signal was defined as a measure of the inflation trend using market-based indicators. The authors explicitly stated their methodology: "To measure how much monetary inflation actually reaches the real economy, we utilize a number of market-based indicators," and they referred to this resulting measure as the “Incrementum Inflation Signal.”

The approach evolved in **2018** as the focus shifted to actively monitoring the financial markets to determine the presence of an inflationary environment. The analysis moved from merely defining the signal to actively monitoring its status. During this period, the methodology involved monitoring specific indicators: "we monitor special indicators on the financial markets from which we distill the Incrementum Inflation Signal." The application of this signal was demonstrated, as the analysis indicated a specific state: "At the moment, our inflation signal indicates a rising inflation tendency at a signal strength of 50%."

By **2020**, the concept matured into a practical tool for portfolio management. The signal was refined into a proprietary indicator specifically designed to guide investment choices. The goal was to use this derived measure to inform portfolio construction. The final iteration of the concept established its utility for decision-making: "We have therefore developed a proprietary inflation signal with which we analyse the current inflation trend." Ultimately, the inflation signal obtained serves as the direct basis for strategic action, as "The inflation signal thus obtained is a basis for our asset allocation decisions."
