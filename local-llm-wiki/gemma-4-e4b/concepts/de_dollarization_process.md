# De-dollarization Process

The process of de-dollarization describes the gradual, long-term shift away from the singular dominance of the US dollar in the global monetary system toward a more decentralized or gold-referenced architecture. This concept is central to understanding the evolving global financial landscape, suggesting a fundamental reorganization of how value is measured and exchanged worldwide.

## How the argument evolved

The initial framing of this concept, as seen in **2017**, focused on a clear directional movement: a "process of de-dollarization" where the world was moving toward a system referencing gold, heralded by the phrase "Hello Gold." This perspective suggested a definitive break from the existing order. By **2018**, the narrative matured, describing this shift not as a sudden event, but as a "reorganization of an outdated monetary architecture," specifically charting the transition "From the Dollar to Gold Via the Yuan and the Euro," solidifying the view of it as a protracted structural change.

The pace of this transition was further analyzed in **2020**, where the report emphasized the persistence of the trend, noting, "We have been observing the long-running, creeping process of 'de-dollarization,'" and positing that this slow movement points toward "a slow path to a multipolar world." However, the perspective became more nuanced by **2024**. While the process itself is acknowledged as having continued "for many years," the report tempered expectations, maintaining that despite the ongoing shift, "we do not believe that this process will lead to the US dollar being replaced," concluding that "the 'one-eyed' US dollar is king for the time being."
