# Gold Price Technical Analysis

Gold Price Technical Analysis involves applying various charting patterns and momentum indicators to determine the prevailing trend and potential entry or exit points for gold investors. This methodology serves as a critical lens through which the reports assess the market's immediate health and long-term trajectory, suggesting whether the market is poised for continuation or reversal.

## How the argument evolved

The application of technical analysis in the reports shows a clear progression in the assessment of market conditions over the years. In **2011**, the prevailing view was highly bullish, with indicators suggesting "an intact bull market," leading the authors to conclude that there were "buying opportunity[s] within an intact upward trend," as "The long-term chart indicates an intact bull market."

By **2012**, the focus remained on trend identification, though the language became more nuanced as the market navigated corrections. The analysis continued to apply "technical analysis of the gold price," noting that while corrections were present, "It seems that the correction is now coming to an end," and that the price development had not yet "overshot" on the logarithmic scale.

The perspective sharpened significantly in **2014**, where the focus shifted heavily toward identifying extreme conditions. The analysis highlighted a state of deep undervaluation, noting that the "1 year oscillator: Gold massively oversold at the moment," and that the gold was "currently extremely oversold."

The most recent iteration in **2021** continued the application of "technical analysis of the gold price," but broadened the toolkit. The reports began referencing specific, established patterns, such as the "long-term cup-handle formation," and brought in momentum indicators like the "Coppock curve, a reliable momentum indicator," to guide the investment thesis.
