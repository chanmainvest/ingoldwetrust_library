# Inflationary vs Deflationary Forces

The investment landscape is perpetually defined by a dynamic tension between forces that drive prices upward (inflation) and those that drive them downward (deflation). This constant "power struggle between market-cleansing natural deflation and politically-induced inflation" dictates the underlying health and trajectory of monetary systems. Understanding this balance is crucial, as the outcome of this struggle determines the viability of assets like gold.

## How the argument evolved

In **2014**, the report framed the market environment as one where "Currently various potentially inflationary as well as deflationary forces are offsetting each other." The focus was on this ongoing, active contest within the system. The narrative suggested that the market was caught in a state where these opposing forces were actively balancing each other out.

By **2015**, the examination deepened, moving beyond mere offsetting forces to consider the potential victors in this monetary contest. The report continued to examine "the potential outcomes of monetary struggles between inflation and deflation." While the forces remained in conflict, the tone suggested a leaning toward the upside for gold. The conclusion in **2015** was that "it is not a given whether inflationary or deflationary forces will be victorious," yet it strongly implied a preference, noting that "higher price inflation will definitely be favored over a deflationary cleansing." This evolution shows a shift from merely describing the forces at play to assessing which side is more likely to prevail in the long run.
