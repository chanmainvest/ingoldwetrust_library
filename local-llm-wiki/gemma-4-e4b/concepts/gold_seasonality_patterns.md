# Gold Seasonality Patterns

Gold exhibits predictable, yet nuanced, seasonal patterns that influence its performance across different timeframes. These patterns suggest that certain months, quarters, or even weekdays possess inherent tendencies that can be leveraged in investment strategy. Understanding these cyclical tendencies is crucial for investors seeking to anticipate short-to-medium term directional biases within the precious metal market.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of seasonality in the reports shows a clear evolution in specificity between the two years examined. In **2018**, the focus was on broader, longer-term monthly trends, noting that "Gold typically rises from the beginning of July to the end of February." Beyond the monthly cycle, the analysis also highlighted specific intra-week biases, pointing out that "The weekday that stands out in terms of performance is Friday."

By **2020**, the analysis became significantly more granular, shifting attention toward specific mid-month windows rather than just broad monthly arcs. The narrative moved from general monthly trends to identifying distinct "tailwinds." The **2020** report noted that "seasonal tailwinds set in from mid-May but are reversed at the beginning of July," suggesting a more complex interplay of forces at play. Furthermore, the concept of recurring strong phases was emphasized, as the report mentioned that "another seasonally strong phase begins in mid-September," adding another layer of temporal specificity compared to the earlier focus on the July-February cycle.
