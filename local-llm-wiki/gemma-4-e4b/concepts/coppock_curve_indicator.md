# Coppock Curve Indicator

The Coppock curve functions as a long-term momentum indicator designed to signal significant shifts in market trends. It is viewed as a "very long term oriented and reliable momentum indicator" that possesses the capacity to "reliably signals large-scale trend changes" within the investment landscape.

## How the argument evolved

The treatment of the Coppock curve within the reports shows a consistent focus on its utility as a trend-signaling mechanism across the years covered. In **2017**, the indicator was established as a tool for identifying major directional shifts, described as a "Coppock curve, a very long term oriented and reliable momentum indicator." The emphasis was placed on its ability to detect these large-scale movements within the market structure.

By **2018**, the discussion revisited the indicator, maintaining its status as a key tool for discerning market direction. The report noted that "we are going to have a look at the Coppock curve again, which is a reliable momentum indicator." The focus remained on its reliability in signaling trend reversals, though the specific actionable signal was refined. The document noted that a key signal occurs "If the indicator turns upwards below the zero axis," providing a specific condition for interpreting the curve's movement. Despite the passage of time, the core belief in the indicator's power to map major market cycles remained constant.
