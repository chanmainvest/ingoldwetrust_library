# Gold Oil Ratio Trends

The gold-to-oil ratio serves as a critical barometer for assessing the underlying health of global financial systems, particularly concerning the petrodollar dynamic. This ratio provides insight into whether the prevailing economic conditions are trending toward stability or instability. Ultimately, it helps investors gauge the structural integrity of the relationship between hard commodities and precious metals.

## How the argument evolved

The examination of this ratio evolved significantly between the two reports, shifting from a focus on immediate economic cycles to a broader geopolitical assessment of currency dominance. In **2009**, the focus was heavily rooted in technical market signals, suggesting that the ratio was a direct indicator of the immediate economic climate. At that time, the prevailing view was that "Oil and gold have a strong positive correlation," and the ratio itself was interpreted through the lens of cyclical market health. Specifically, the reports noted that "An increasing gold/oil ratio tends to go hand in hand with recessions," while conversely, "a falling ratio tends to indicate economic prosperity and stability" [2009 — 27 Technical Analysis].

By **2018**, the narrative broadened considerably, moving away from simple recessionary signals toward a structural critique of global finance. The ratio was reframed as a measure of the petrodollar system's vitality. The core idea became that the ratio itself "tells you something about the health" of this system [2018 — 07 De Dollarization From The Dollar To Gold Via The Yuan And The Euro]. The evidence presented in **2018** suggested that when the "gold-to-oil ratio rises," it signals specific pressures within the global financial architecture, offering a different lens than the purely cyclical view of **2009** [2018 — 07 De Dollarization From The Dollar To Gold Via The Yuan And The Euro]. Furthermore, the ratio was quantified in terms of commodity exchange, referencing benchmarks such as "13 barrels of oil to buy an ounce of gold" [2018 — 07 De Dollarization From The Dollar To Gold Via The Yuan And The Euro].
