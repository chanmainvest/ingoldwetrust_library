# Technical Analysis Focus

Technical analysis, as treated within the *In Gold We Trust* corpus, is defined as a methodology that must remain strictly tethered to observable market mechanics rather than underlying economic theories. The core tenet is that successful trading decisions should be derived solely from the patterns visible in price action and market structure, maintaining a necessary distance from fundamental economic viewpoints.

## How the argument evolved

The emphasis on the purity of technical analysis remained remarkably consistent across the reports, though the phrasing evolved slightly between the years. In **2016**, the guidance was clear: practitioners should "focus exclusively on technical conditions in terms of price and market structure," ensuring this approach remained "independent of one's view of the fundamentals." This established the principle that the technical reading must be self-contained.

By **2017**, the report reinforced this dedication to structural observation. The directive remained firm, urging readers to "focus exclusively on technical conditions and market structure," thereby demanding that the analysis proceed "independent of one's fundamental opinions." While the language tightened slightly, the underlying message—that technical signals are a self-sufficient domain—was fully preserved from the previous year. The consistent insistence on this methodological separation suggests that the report views technical analysis as a discipline requiring rigorous adherence to its own visual evidence, regardless of whether the market is driven by inflation or monetary policy.
