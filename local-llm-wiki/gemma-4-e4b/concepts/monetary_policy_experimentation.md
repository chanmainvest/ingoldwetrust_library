# Monetary Policy Experimentation

The global financial system is currently situated within a prolonged period of monetary policy experimentation, a state that has persisted for years following the near collapse of the system in late 2008. This ongoing experimentation defines the prevailing economic environment, suggesting that policymakers are still navigating uncharted territory in their attempts to manage global liquidity and stability.

## How the argument evolved

The reports frame the current economic climate as one defined by sustained policy trials. The foundational view established in **2015** is that "we are now in the 7th year of global monetary policy experimentation," a situation that arose "Since the narrowly averted implosion of the financial system in the fall of 2008." This framing suggests a long, drawn-out process of policy adjustment rather than a singular event.

The perspective solidified in **2015** continued to characterize the environment as one of ongoing trials. The narrative emphasizes that the current state is "In a world of monetary policy experiments," specifically referencing the continuation of certain mechanisms. This viewpoint highlights the persistence of policies such as "discontinuing QE and the zero interest rate policy," underscoring that the experimental phase is not merely theoretical but is actively being managed in the markets. The consistent thread across the reports is that the financial system remains in a state of active, extended policy testing.
