# Technical Analysis Assessment

The Technical Analysis Assessment within the *In Gold We Trust* corpus serves as a critical mechanism for applying quantitative methodologies to the gold market. It is designed to move beyond purely fundamental arguments by subjecting the gold price to rigorous examination using established market indicators and structural patterns. This assessment is significant because it attempts to distill complex market movements into actionable signals regarding prevailing trends and sentiment.

## How the argument evolved

The application of technical analysis in the reports shows a consistent focus on applying established patterns to the gold price. In **2022**, the report detailed its methodology, applying "technical analysis of the gold price" through the use of various indicators and patterns. This analysis was framed as a comprehensive "analysis of market structure, sentiment, and price patterns," highlighting specific tools such as the "Coppock curve, which has proved to be a reliable momentum indicator."

By **2023**, the report continued to utilize this framework, though the tone of the assessment became more nuanced. The **2023** iteration noted that "The analysis of market structure, sentiment and price patterns leads us to a rather mixed technical assessment." Despite this mixed signal, the report maintained a directional view, suggesting that "the medium-term trend is certainly in favor of gold at the moment." Thus, the evolution shows a consistent application of technical tools, moving from a detailed presentation of indicators in **2022** to a more tempered, yet still directional, conclusion in **2023**.
