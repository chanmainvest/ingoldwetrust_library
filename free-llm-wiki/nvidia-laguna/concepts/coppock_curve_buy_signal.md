# Coppock Curve Buy Signal

The Coppock Curve is a long-term momentum indicator that signals bullish trends in gold prices when it crosses above the zero line. This technical tool has consistently identified favorable entry points for gold investments, with reports from 2017 through 2022 documenting its predictive value. The indicator's significance lies in its ability to capture sustained upward momentum, making it a reliable guide for long-term gold allocation decisions.

## How the argument evolved

**2017** marked an important confirmation point for the Coppock curve's bullish signal, as the indicator "gave a buy signal in early 2015" and then "In the beginning of 2017, the Coppock crossed the zero-line," reinforcing the long-term upward trend for gold. The report emphasized that "a buy signal is given when the indicator turns up from below the zero line," establishing the foundational principle for interpreting the indicator's signals.

**2018** continued the narrative by noting that "The indicator switched to buy at the beginning of 2014" and then "At the beginning of 2018, the Coppock curve successfully rebounded from the zero line," demonstrating the indicator's role in confirming bullish setups after periods of being in the buy zone. The report clarified the mechanics: "If the indicator turns upwards below the zero axis, i.e. assumes a positive slope, this is seen as a buy signal."

**2019** provided a more sustained perspective, stating that "The indicator has been on a buy signal since the end of 2015," indicating a prolonged bullish trend for gold. The report maintained the same core definition: "A buy signal is given when the indicator turns up below the zero line," showing consistency in the analytical framework.

**2020** reinforced the continuity of the signal, noting that "Since the end of 2015, the indicator has been on a 'buy'" and emphasizing that this indicated "a positive long-term momentum for gold." The definition remained consistent: "A buy signal is given when the indicator turns upward."

**2021** continued the pattern, observing that "Since the end of 2015 the indicator has been on 'buy'" and remained in positive territory. The report specified the precise condition: "A buy signal occurs when the indicator, which is below the zero line, turns upwards."

**2022** provided the most recent update, confirming that "Since the end of 2015, the indicator has been on 'buy'" but also noting a subtle development: "since December 2021 it is now back on an upward trend," suggesting the indicator had recently reinforced its bullish signal after a brief southward movement.
