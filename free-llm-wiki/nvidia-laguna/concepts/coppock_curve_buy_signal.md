# Coppock Curve Buy Signal

The Coppock Curve is a long-term momentum indicator that signals bullish trends in gold prices when it crosses above the zero line. This technical analysis tool has consistently identified favorable entry points for gold investments, with reports from 2017 through 2022 documenting its predictive value. The indicator's significance lies in its ability to capture sustained upward momentum, making it a reliable guide for long-term gold allocation decisions.

## How the argument evolved

**2017** marked an important confirmation point for the Coppock curve's bullish signal, as the indicator "crossed the zero-line in early 2017," validating the earlier buy signal from early 2015. The report emphasized that "a buy signal is given when the indicator turns up from below the zero line," establishing the foundational principle for interpreting the indicator's movements.

In **2018**, the analysis deepened by noting that the Coppock curve had "switched to buy at the beginning of 2014" and then "successfully rebounded from the zero line" at the start of 2018. This year's report clarified the mechanics: "If the indicator turns upwards below the zero axis, i.e. assumes a positive slope, this is seen as a buy signal," reinforcing the technical criteria for signal generation.

The **2019** report consolidated the timeline, stating that "the indicator has been on a buy signal since the end of 2015," suggesting a remarkably persistent bullish phase for gold. The consistency of the signal was emphasized through the repeated definition: "A buy signal is given when the indicator turns up below the zero line."

**2020** continued this narrative, confirming that "since the end of 2015, the indicator has been on a 'buy'" and describing it as indicating "a positive long-term momentum for gold." The report maintained the core definition while highlighting the extended duration of the bullish signal.

The **2021** analysis reinforced the established pattern, noting that "since the end of 2015 the indicator has been on 'buy'" and remained "in positive territory." The technical criteria were restated with precision: "A buy signal occurs when the indicator, which is below the zero line, turns upwards."

**2022** provided the most recent update, confirming the signal had persisted "since the end of 2015" and noting a technical development: "since December 2021 it is now back on an upward trend." This year's report acknowledged a brief southward movement before the recent upward turn, demonstrating the indicator's sensitivity to subtle momentum shifts while maintaining the long-term bullish interpretation.
