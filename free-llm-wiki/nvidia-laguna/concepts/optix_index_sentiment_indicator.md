# Optix Index Sentiment Indicator

The Optix Index Sentiment Indicator is a contrarian sentiment gauge that aggregates survey data and futures positioning to identify potential turning points in gold market psychology. By tracking the balance between bullish and bearish sentiment among market participants, this indicator serves as a key tool for timing entries and exits in gold-related investments. Its significance lies in its ability to highlight periods of excessive pessimism or optimism, which historically have preceded notable price movements in the gold market.

## How the argument evolved

**2017** marked the initial introduction of the Optix Index as a favored sentiment metric, with the report emphasizing its contrarian nature. The analysis noted that "One of our favorite sentiment indicators is the Optix Index," highlighting how "excessive pessimism is actually positive" for investors. At that time, the index was observed to "stand close to levels that often coincided with good buying opportunities," suggesting that extreme bearish sentiment could signal a favorable entry point for contrarian investors.

By **2022**, the Optix Index had evolved to reflect a more nuanced market environment, registering at 49 points and entering "neutral territory." The report stated, "Currently, the Optix Index is at 49 and thus in neutral territory," while reinforcing that "This confirms our assessment that sentiment in the sector is basically constructive." However, the analysis also cautioned that "If the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it is time to become more cautious," introducing a clear threshold for heightened bullish sentiment that might warrant defensive positioning.

In **2023**, the indicator continued to provide valuable insights, with the monthly Optix reading at 61 points—still within the neutral range. The report observed, "Currently, the monthly Optix is at 61 and thus still in neutral territory," but added a critical technical observation: "Comparing the current level with the last peak in July 2020, a clear positive divergence can be seen." This divergence suggested that while sentiment remained balanced, underlying momentum was building, pointing toward potential upward movement in gold prices.
