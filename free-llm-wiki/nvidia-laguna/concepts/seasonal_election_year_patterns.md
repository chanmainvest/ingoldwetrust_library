# Seasonal Election Year Patterns

Seasonal election year patterns refer to recurring price movements in gold and other financial assets that align with the political calendar, particularly during election cycles. These patterns emerge from the interplay of investor behavior, market sentiment, and macroeconomic conditions surrounding key electoral events. Understanding these seasonal tendencies is crucial for anticipating short-term market dynamics and positioning portfolios accordingly, as they often manifest as predictable phases of strength and weakness in asset prices.

## How the argument evolved

**2022** — The 2022 report first identified a distinct seasonal pattern in gold prices during midterm election years, noting that "seasonal headwinds set in from the end of May, but reverse again at the beginning of July." This observation highlighted a specific window of weakness extending from late May through June, with the report emphasizing that "June is, after May, the second weakest month in election years." The analysis suggested that these seasonal pressures could persist into the coming weeks, advising readers to anticipate "further headwinds" during this period.

**2024** — Building on the earlier framework, the 2024 report expanded the seasonal analysis to include both gold and silver, while also broadening the timeframe to encompass presidential election years. It documented that "a seasonally weaker phase begins in July and only ends in November," indicating a more extended period of seasonal weakness compared to the midterm pattern. The report further observed that "A seasonal downward trend can also be seen in silver from August to the end of November," suggesting that this seasonal dynamic affects multiple precious metals and persists through the election period.
