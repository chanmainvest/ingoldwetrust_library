# Gold Price Technical Analysis

Gold price technical analysis is the practice of using charts, indicators, and historical price patterns to forecast future movements in the precious metal. Within the annual "In Gold We Trust" reports, this analytical framework has evolved alongside gold's price trajectory, reflecting changing market dynamics and investor sentiment. The reports consistently employ technical tools to contextualize gold's performance, offering readers a structured lens through which to understand the metal's price behavior and potential future paths.

## How the argument evolved

**2007** marked an early phase where the report identified a clear and enduring upward trajectory for gold. The analysis highlighted an "intact upward trend since 2002," with the first target price set at "last year's high of USD 730" and a subsequent resistance line anticipated at "the all-time-high of USD 875." This bullish technical outlook underscored gold's momentum during a period of rising commodity prices and growing macroeconomic uncertainty.

**2017** introduced a more nuanced and range-bound perspective. The report noted a "stochastics sell signal on the weekly chart," suggesting a potential shift from the earlier bullish phase. Analysts observed that "gold is likely to remain range-bound between the USD 1,200 and 1,290 levels," indicating consolidation after a prolonged upswing. The discussion incorporated moving averages, with attention drawn to "a final retest of the 200-day moving average" as a key technical event that could signal either a breakout or a deeper correction.

**2023** brought renewed focus to technical patterns amid a new macro environment. The analysis identified "ideal conditions in the medium term for a sustained breakout above the major resistance zone around USD 2,050," reflecting optimism about a potential new upward move. However, the report also acknowledged that "gold has been getting more and more stuck around the USD 2,050 mark," suggesting persistent selling pressure. A notable development was the identification of "a bearish wedge has formed," a pattern often associated with impending downward momentum, adding a layer of caution to the otherwise bullish technical setup.
