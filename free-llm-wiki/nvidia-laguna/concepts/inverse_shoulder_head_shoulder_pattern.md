# Inverse Shoulder-Head-Shoulder Pattern

The inverse shoulder-head-shoulder pattern is a bearish reversal chart pattern that signals a potential bottom in market prices. In the context of gold analysis, this formation has been used by the In Gold We Trust reports to project future price targets, serving as a technical indicator for when the metal might reverse its corrective trajectory and resume an upward move. The pattern's significance lies in its reliability as a reversal signal, with the 2010 and 2018 reports demonstrating its predictive value in forecasting gold's trajectory.

## How the argument evolved

**2010** marked the first documented instance of this pattern emerging in the reports' analysis. During a market correction, the authors noted that "inverse shoulder-head-shoulder formation emerged during the correction" and identified a target price of USD 1,300. This early recognition of the pattern demonstrated the report's commitment to technical analysis as a tool for understanding gold's price movements, particularly during periods of market stress.

The pattern re-emerged in **2018** with even greater emphasis on its potential. The reports described gold as having established "an impressive inverse shoulder-head-shoulder formation that could be resolved upwards," with a target of USD 1,800. The language used in 2018 was more definitive, suggesting that if "the gold price were to break through this resistance zone, the next target would be USD 1,800." This evolution from a bearish signal in 2010 to a bullish reversal indicator in 2018 reflects the changing market dynamics and the pattern's adaptability as a forecasting tool across different market cycles.
