# Gold as Recession Hedge

Gold has long been regarded as a critical asset class for investors navigating economic downturns, offering both protection against market volatility and a reliable store of value when uncertainty looms. Its enduring appeal during recessions stems from its ability to preserve purchasing power and its historical track record of outperforming equities in times of financial stress. As central banks grapple with shifting monetary policies and inflationary pressures, the role of gold as a recession hedge remains a cornerstone of prudent portfolio allocation.

## How the argument evolved

**2023** — The 2023 edition of *In Gold We Trust* reinforced gold’s reputation as a defensive asset by highlighting its consistent outperformance during the early stages of economic downturns. The report emphasized that "gold price rises on average 10.6% during a recession," underscoring its statistical reliability as a hedge. It further noted that "gold performs positively in all phases of the recession," positioning it as a stabilizing force when equity markets wobble. Importantly, the analysis revealed that "gold tends to outperform equities in the first three phases," suggesting its particular strength during the initial turbulence of a downturn.

**2024** — Building on this foundation, the 2024 report reaffirmed gold’s historical resilience, describing it as having "typically been an excellent portfolio component during recessions." The introduction framed gold not just as a reactive asset but as a strategic one, capable of cushioning portfolios when "a slide in equity markets would probably be expected in the event of a significant slowdown in the US economy." This continuity in messaging reflects a growing consensus: gold’s value is most pronounced precisely when traditional assets falter, making it an indispensable tool for risk management.
