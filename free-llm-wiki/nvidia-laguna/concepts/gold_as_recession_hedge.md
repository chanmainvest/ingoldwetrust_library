# Gold as Recession Hedge

Gold has long been regarded as a critical safeguard for investors navigating economic downturns, offering both protection against market volatility and a reliable store of value when confidence wavers. Its enduring appeal during recessions stems from its ability to preserve purchasing power and provide stability in uncertain financial landscapes. Across multiple editions of the *In Gold We Trust* report, the metal’s role as a recession hedge has been consistently validated, with evidence showing its outperformance relative to equities and its positive returns across all phases of economic contraction.

## How the argument evolved

**2023** — 08 The Showdown In Monetary Policy marked a pivotal moment in reinforcing gold’s defensive credentials. The report underscored that gold consistently outperforms equities in early recession phases, positioning it as a reliable hedge against uncertainty. It highlighted that "gold price rises on average 10.6% during a recession" and emphasized that "gold performs positively in all phases of the recession." Furthermore, the analysis noted that "gold tends to outperform equities in the first three phases," illustrating its resilience even as broader markets falter.

Building on this foundation, **2024** — 01 Introduction expanded the narrative by framing gold as a portfolio stabilizer during equity downturns. The report stated that "gold has typically been an excellent portfolio component during recessions" and linked its performance directly to macroeconomic conditions, observing that "A slide in equity markets would probably be expected in the event of a significant slowdown in the US economy." This continuity in messaging across years demonstrates how gold’s reputation as a recession hedge has solidified through empirical evidence and strategic analysis.

Together, these chapters trace the evolution of gold’s role from a speculative asset to a cornerstone of prudent portfolio management during times of economic stress.
