# Gold as Monetary Asset

Gold's transformation from mere commodity to recognized monetary asset represents one of the most profound shifts in global financial perception during the early 2010s. This evolution reflects a growing consensus that gold's intrinsic scarcity and historical role as currency position it uniquely against increasingly debased paper money. The concept's significance lies in its challenge to conventional asset classification, positioning gold not as a mere investment but as a foundational monetary instrument.

## How the argument evolved

**2011** marked the initial articulation of gold's monetary character in the In Gold We Trust corpus. The report established that "gold has been more and more regarded as monetary," signaling a departure from traditional commodity-based analysis. This perspective emphasized gold's evolving status beyond mere physical metal, noting its formal recognition as "currency code (XAU)" in international financial systems. The introduction explicitly framed gold through the lens of "monetary character of gold," suggesting that market participants were beginning to view it through the same fundamental principles that govern currency valuation.

**2015** saw this monetary framing become more assertive and analytical. The report declared its approach explicitly: "We analyze gold primarily as a monetary asset and not as a commodity." This represented a significant evolution from the 2011 observation to a deliberate analytical choice. More importantly, the 2015 introduction quantified the shift, noting that "The competitive position of gold relative to paper money and other asset classes has improved considerably." This improvement was attributed to the growing recognition of paper money's limitations and the public's increasing preference for assets with intrinsic value. The progression from 2011's descriptive observation to 2015's analytical framework demonstrates how the report's treatment matured from documenting a trend to actively analyzing gold's strengthened monetary fundamentals.
