# Midas Touch Gold Model Signals

The Midas Touch Gold Model is a sophisticated trend-following framework that generates investment signals for gold by analyzing the performance dynamics of key global currencies, mining equities, and monetary conditions. Its significance lies in synthesizing diverse macroeconomic indicators—ranging from the Chinese renminbi and Indian Rupee to the GDX mining ETF, US dollar strength, and real interest rates—into a cohesive system that helps investors navigate gold's complex market behavior. By combining technical analysis with fundamental monetary data, the model provides actionable insights into when gold is positioned to outperform traditional assets.

## How the argument evolved

**2018** established the foundational architecture of the Midas Touch Gold Model, introducing a multi-dimensional approach to gold signal generation. The model initially relied on trend-following signals derived from the price performance of the Chinese renminbi and the Indian Rupee, recognizing that these emerging market currencies often move in tandem with gold during periods of global risk sentiment. As the report noted, "two trend-following signals based on the price performance of the Chinese renminbi and the Indian Rupee" formed a critical component of the analytical framework. Additionally, the model incorporated sentiment analysis through the mining ETF GDX, with the team observing that "we use a trend-following chart of the mining ETF GDX and the according sentiment" to capture equity market dynamics that could influence gold's trajectory. The US dollar's performance on forward markets and the time series of US real interest rates were also integrated as complementary indicators, acknowledging that "The model is complemented by US dollar performance data and the constellation of the US currency on the forward market as well as by the time series of real interest rates in the USA."

By **2023**, the model had matured into a more refined signal generator, demonstrating its predictive power through concrete market calls. On March 10th, 2023, the Midas Touch Gold Model™ executed a significant shift, transitioning from a neutral stance to a bullish outlook on gold. This change was accompanied by active buy signals on both the monthly and weekly charts, indicating sustained upward momentum. The report emphasized that "The Midas Touch Gold Model™ switched from neutral to bullish on March 10th, 2023" and confirmed that "Both the monthly and the weekly charts still have active buy signals," showcasing the model's ability to adapt to evolving market conditions and provide timely investment guidance.
