# Technical Analysis as Investment Tool

Technical analysis is a methodology used by investors to analyze and forecast market trends through the study of past price data and trading volumes. Within the context of gold investment, it serves as a complementary approach to fundamental analysis, helping investors identify optimal entry and exit points. The In Gold We Trust reports have consistently emphasized the value of technical analysis as a practical tool for timing and location decisions in gold markets.

## How the argument evolved

**2019** marked the initial formal recognition of technical analysis as a valuable component of the investment decision-making process. The report positioned it as a tool for determining both timing and location, stating that "technical analysis is certainly not an exact science, but rather a useful tool." This acknowledgment came alongside a broader emphasis on understanding markets from multiple perspectives, noting it was "important to us not only to understand the 'big picture' fundamentally... but also from a technical point of view." The 2019 report further highlighted how technical indicators were signaling positive conditions, observing that "conditions for the establishment of the new bull market seem excellent from a technical point of view."

By **2021**, the report's treatment of technical analysis had evolved to emphasize its role in determining investment location and timing more explicitly. The language became more direct, stating "technical analysis is a useful tool for determining the location and timing of investments." This evolution reflected a maturation of the argument, moving from a general endorsement to a more strategic application. The 2021 report maintained the dual-perspective approach, emphasizing the importance to "understand the 'big picture' not only from a fundamental but also from a technical perspective." This consistent framing across years demonstrates how the reports have integrated technical analysis as a core component of their analytical framework.
