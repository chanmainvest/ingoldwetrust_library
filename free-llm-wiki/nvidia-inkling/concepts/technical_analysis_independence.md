# Technical Analysis Independence

Technical conditions in price and market structure are assessed objectively and can diverge from fundamental conclusions.

## Analysis

### 2016 — 46 Technical Analysis
Technical conditions in price and market structure are assessed objectively and can diverge from fundamental conclusions.
Evidence from source:
> focus exclusively on technical conditions in terms of price and market structure
> objective assessment of those factors independent of one's view of the fundamentals
> Sometimes technical analysis can lead an analyst to completely different conclusions

### 2017 — 12 Technical Analysis
Technical analysis should focus exclusively on market structure and be objective, independent of fundamental opinions, sometimes yielding different conclusions.
Evidence from source:
> focus exclusively on technical conditions and market structure
> analyze the situation objectively and independent of one's fundamental opinions
> arrive at an entirely different conclusion based on his analysis of the technical picture
