# Cup-Handle Formation Target

The cup-handle formation target is a long-term technical pattern applied to gold, defining a measured bullish price objective near USD 2,700 once the consolidation resolves to the upside. Rooted in the correction that began in August 2020, the setup reinterprets that prolonged drawdown not as a bearish reversal but as the "handle" of a multi-year cup, giving analysts a concrete structural milestone. Its significance in the In Gold We Trust corpus is that it transforms an ambiguous consolidation into a quantifiable resolution scenario, anchoring expectations to a specific breakout level across multiple report cycles.

## How the argument evolved

**2021** — In the technical analysis chapter of the 2021 report, the pattern was first explicitly mapped onto gold's price action following the August 2020 peak. The analysts observed that "The correction since August 2020 forms a cup-handle pattern with a measured price target near USD 2,700 upon breakout," framing the post-peak weakness as constructive rather than destructive. At this stage the formation was still developing, yet the report already pointed to an imminent resolution, noting that "The long-term cup-handle formation, which could now soon be resolved" was in place. The quantitative implication was unambiguous: "Accordingly, the price target of the formation is around USD 2,700." By establishing both the structural identity of the correction and the precise measured move, the 2021 analysis laid the foundational thesis—that the handle portion of the cup was being carved out in real time, with a breakout promising to reactivate the broader uptrend.

**2023** — Two years later, the 2023 technical analysis chapter returned to the same formation not to revise it, but to confirm its endurance and refine its interpretation. The report reiterated that "The long-term cup-handle formation, which could now soon be resolved, appears particularly interesting," suggesting that the pattern had survived the intervening volatility and remained technically valid. The structural narrative was made explicit: "The correction since August 2020 appears as the handle part of the formation," reinforcing the continuity between the initial 2020 peak, the subsequent consolidation, and the present setup. Most tellingly, the price objective had not drifted; "The price target of the formation remains at around USD 2,700," underscoring the stability of the measured move despite the passage of time. Where the 2021 chapter introduced the cup-handle as an emerging possibility, the 2023 chapter treated it as a persistent, unresolved framework—one whose handle had been fully formed since August 2020 and whose resolution, when it came, would still point toward the same USD 2,700 target.

Taken together, the two chapters present a rare consistency in technical forecasting: the identification of a long-term pattern, the assignment of a specific price target, and the sustained validation of that target across report cycles. Rather than abandoning the formation as market conditions evolved, the report deepened its commitment to the interpretation, allowing the "handle" narrative to mature from an initial observation into a durable analytical pillar.
