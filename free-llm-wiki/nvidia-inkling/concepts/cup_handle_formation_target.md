# Cup-Handle Formation Target

The cup-handle formation target is a technical-analysis concept that interprets gold's price consolidation since August 2020 as the "handle" of a long-term cup-shaped base, projecting a measured breakout objective near USD 2,700. In the In Gold We Trust corpus, this pattern serves as a structural benchmark for assessing whether the correction is resolving and the broader bull market is resuming. Its significance lies in offering a quantifiable, chart-derived price target—around USD 2,700—that remains stable across reports, providing an analytical anchor distinct from macroeconomic storytelling.

## How the argument evolved

**2021** introduced the framework by identifying the post-August 2020 correction as the developing handle of a larger cup-handle formation. Rather than treating the drawdown as an isolated event, the report embedded it within classical chart geometry, observing that "The long-term cup-handle formation, which could now soon be resolved" was approaching a decisive phase. The analysis paired this structural view with a specific measured move, stating that "Accordingly, the price target of the formation is around USD 2,700." At this stage, the pattern was presented as an imminent setup: the handle was still forming, the breakout had not yet occurred, and the USD 2,700 level stood as the projected reward upon resolution.

By **2023**, the narrative had shifted from anticipation to structural confirmation without altering the core thesis. The report reiterated that "The long-term cup-handle formation, which could now soon be resolved, appears particularly interesting," preserving the language of impending resolution first introduced two years prior. Crucially, the 2023 chapter clarified the pattern's anatomy, noting that "The correction since August 2020 appears as the handle part of the formation," thereby explicitly linking the multi-year consolidation to the cup-handle's geometry. Even as the timeline for resolution extended, the quantitative objective held firm: "The price target of the formation remains at around USD 2,700." This continuity demonstrates how the technical argument evolved—not by revising its price target, but by deepening the structural narrative, transforming an emerging pattern into a confirmed, long-duration formation whose eventual breakout could still deliver the originally projected USD 2,700 objective.
