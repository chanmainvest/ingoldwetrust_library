# Rothbard's Three Inflation Phases

Rothbard outlines three inflation phases ending in currency abandonment and crack-up boom.

## Analysis

### 2015 — 03 Gold And Inflation
Rothbard outlines three inflation phases ending in currency abandonment and crack-up boom.
Evidence from source:
> Rothbard defines the desperate fight against deflation
> In the first phase, the money supply is increased, but prices barely rise
> Phase three is commonly called the “crack-up boom”
> People abandon the currency system in a concerned mood

### 2020 — 07 Status Quo Of Inflation Dynamics
Rothbard outlines three inflation phases ending in hyperinflation, where public expectation shifts and demand for money collapses.
Evidence from source:
> In the first phase the money supply is increased, but prices hardly rise at all.
> In the second phase, the public begins to anticipate slowly rising prices.
> Phase 3 is commonly referred to as the “crack-up boom” or “hyperinflation”.
> A frantic rush ensues to get rid of money at all costs and to buy anything else.
