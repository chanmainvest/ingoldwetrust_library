# Gold Seasonality Pattern

Indian wedding and Diwali seasons plus Christmas stocking drive Q4 and Q1 strength, a pattern seen in 75-80% of years.

## Analysis

### 2012 — 18 Technical Analysis Of The Gold Price
Indian wedding and Diwali seasons plus Christmas stocking drive Q4 and Q1 strength, a pattern seen in 75-80% of years.
Evidence from source:
> gold tends to perform best in the fourth and the first quarter
> This seasonality has been observed in 75-80% of the cases.
> The so-called wedding season and the Diwali festival in India result in a seasonal uptick in demand

### 2014 — 34 Technical Analysis Bottoming Phase Almost Complete
Gold displays pronounced seasonality, with June favorable for buying and September the strongest month.
Evidence from source:
> June is traditionally a good time to buy
> September is traditionally the strongest month
> In the second half of the year upward momentum is clearly at its highest

### 2015 — 12 Technical Analysis Are We Close To The Final Stages Of The Bottoming Phase
Gold exhibits clear seasonal trends with the strongest upside momentum traditionally occurring in the second half of the year.
Evidence from source:
> We have already discussed the fact that gold exhibits clear seasonal trends
> Traditionally, June is an excellent time for purchases
> Seasonal upside momentum is clearly at its strongest in the second half of the year

### 2017 — 12 Technical Analysis
Gold prices typically rise from early August to late February, generating the bulk of the 7.5% average annual gain, with the rest flat.
Evidence from source:
> gold prices typically rise from early August to the end of February
> vast bulk of gold's 7.5% average annual price gain is generated in this time period
> seasonal return generated in the remaining 167 days from 21 February until August 6 amounted to a negligible 0.30%

### 2018 — 14 Technical Analysis
Gold exhibits pronounced seasonality, with most average annual gains occurring between July and February, especially in midterm election years.
Evidence from source:
> Gold typically rises from the beginning of July to the end of February
> Almost the entire average annual gain tends to occur during that period
> In years of US midterm elections, which tend to be very positive years as far as the performance of gold is concerned
