# Gold Standard Inflation Protection

The gold standard offered the best protection against inflation with very low volatility.

## Analysis

### 2009 — 20 Is Gold Pricing In The Coming Inflation
The gold standard offered the best protection against inflation with very low volatility.
Evidence from source:
> gold standard is proven to offer the best protection against inflation
> annual inflation from 1879 to 1914 was 0.2% at a volatility of only 2.2%
> Since 1971, volatility has been 2.8%, with average inflation at 4.6%

### 2010 — 03 Inflation Vs Deflation
The gold standard delivered far lower inflation and volatility than the post-1971 Bretton Woods era of fiat currency.
Evidence from source:
> The gold standard is proven to offer the best protection against inflation
> annual price inflation from 1879 to 1914 was 0.2% at a volatility of only 2.2%
> Since 1971 (i.e. the end of Bretton Woods), volatility has been 2.8%
> with average price inflation at 4.6%
