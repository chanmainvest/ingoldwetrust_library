# Incrementum Inflation Signal

The Incrementum Inflation Signal is a proprietary indicator developed within the *In Gold We Trust* report series to track inflation dynamics through inflation-sensitive assets and guide strategic asset allocation. First introduced as a market-monitoring tool, it translates complex price and asset trends into a readable strength metric that warns of shifting inflationary regimes. Its significance lies in offering a structured, quantitative lens on inflation volatility—one capable of registering gradual build-ups, crisis-driven collapses, and subsequent peaks with equal clarity.

## How the argument evolved

The report’s treatment of inflation measurement began with direct observation of market indicators. **2018** In "Inflation Vs Deflation The Big Showdown," the authors monitored these indicators to produce an inflation signal that, at the time, stood at a "signal strength of 50%." Rather than signaling crisis, it pointed to a gradual shift: "our inflation signal indicates a rising inflation tendency" that had been building since 2016. This early iteration established the core idea—that inflation dynamics could be distilled into a readable strength metric—without yet framing it as a formal allocation framework.

**2020** The methodology gained institutional weight in "Status Quo Of Inflation Dynamics," where the authors formalized the tool. "We have therefore developed a proprietary inflation signal with which we analyse the current inflation trend," they wrote, clarifying that "The inflation signal thus obtained is a basis for our asset allocation decisions." The signal was no longer merely descriptive; it had become prescriptive. Yet the year also delivered its starkest test. "In the course of the Covid-19 crisis the inflation signal has weakened dramatically," reflecting the abrupt deflationary pressures and market dislocations that accompanied the pandemic’s onset. The collapse demonstrated the indicator’s sensitivity to macroeconomic shocks, reinforcing its value as a real-time barometer rather than a static forecast.

**2023** By "Status Quo Of The Inflation Trend," the narrative had moved from crisis suppression to renewed extremity. The authors reaffirmed ownership of the framework—"we have created the Incrementum Inflation Signal"—and reported that "This has been at its maximum level again since the beginning of April 2023." The environment had shifted into turbulence, captured in the observation that "The new inflation volatility regime is also noticeable in the Incrementum Inflation Signal." Where 2018 had shown a modest 50% strength and 2020 had shown dramatic weakening, 2023 presented a signal at its ceiling, warning that inflation dynamics had not only recovered but intensified into a volatile new regime.
