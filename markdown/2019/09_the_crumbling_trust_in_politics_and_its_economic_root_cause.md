#### **In summary:** 

- The positive feedback loop created within the nexus between unhinged central banking and unprecedented levels of globalization is coming to an end. 

- In the West, the outlet has and will continue to be political in nature. Unconstrained central banking depleted a rich capital base, or pool of real savings, to such an extent that it reduced Western workers’ productivity, _real_ wages and by extension purchasing power.
