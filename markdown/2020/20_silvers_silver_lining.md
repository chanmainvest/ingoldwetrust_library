#### **Silver investors have several supply constraints working in their favor:** 

Firstly, silver mine supply is price inelastic, because 75% of it comes from nonsilver mines (e.g. copper, gold).<sup>**377**</sup> When the price of silver rises, the mines that produce the supermajority of the white metal will not automatically make it their priority to increase supply. 

_Humanity appreciates truth about as much as a squirrel appreciates silver._
