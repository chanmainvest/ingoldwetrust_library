## **d. Commitment of Traders Report**<sup>147</sup> 

##### **Net speculative positioning in gold and silver futures at the COMEX** 

**diverged to a never before seen extent in recent weeks.** The data for gold gradually improved, implying potential market strength going forward, while positioning in silver continued to deteriorate, implying a vulnerable market. Following the strong rally in gold at the beginning of the year, speculators took profits, leading to a corresponding adjustment in the futures market structure. A concomitant rise in the co-basis suggested strengthening physical demand as prices corrected, an indication that continued gold price strength was well supported.

As prices weakened, the net speculative position declined rapidly. Although the offsetting commercial net short position remains relatively large by historical standards, the strong liquidation in such a short period time has definitely cleared the air.
