## **_Ratio analysis: conclusion_** 

The long-term comparison of gold and other asset classes paints a clearly positive picture. While many ratios are close or slightly below the long-term median, this goes to show that the current valuation is certainly not excessive. It is therefore also very easy to rebut the heavily cited argument of the “gold bubble”. We expect the highs of 1980 to be tested or even exceeded, given that the extent of the current debt crisis goes much beyond the situation at the end of the last big bull market. **Bull markets end in euphoria, and this substantiates our argument in favour of an imminent transition to an accelerated trend phase.**
