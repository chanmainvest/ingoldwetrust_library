## **Seasonality** 

### **_Indian wedding season and Christmas as reasons for strong seasonality_** 

One reason for the most recent correction of the gold price is the pronounced seasonality of the price. Said seasonality is not the least due to the so-called wedding season as well as the Diwali Festival in India. Since people in India get married mainly in spring and autumn, the jewellery industry stocks up on material in the third and fourth quarter. The climax and end of the wedding season is May 7th, the day of the Akshaya Tritiya Festival. In addition, jewellers tend to stock up on gold for Christmas in the third and fourth quarter.
