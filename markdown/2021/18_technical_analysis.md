**What is our current technical assessment of the gold price?** For the longterm assessment, we once again refer to the Coppock curve, a reliable momentum indicator.<sup>**484**</sup> A buy signal occurs when the indicator, which is below the zero line, turns upwards, i.e. assumes a positive value. The advantage of this indicator is that you can reliably detect major trend changes. Since the end of 2015 the indicator has been on “buy”. Since summer 2020 the curve has been trending slightly south, but it is still clearly in positive territory. The KST<sup>**485**</sup> confirms the positive longterm setup. 

**The long-term cup-handle formation, which could now soon be resolved, is particularly interesting.** The correction since August 2020 appears as the handle part of the formation. The price target can be estimated by measuring the distance from the right edge of the cup to the bottom of the cup and then extended further in the direction of the breakout area. **Accordingly, the price target of the formation is around USD 2,700.**

### **One of our favorite sentiment indicators is Sentimentrader’s Optix** 

**Index.** This index tracks the most common sentiment indicators as well as data from the futures and options markets. The underlying logic of this barometer is a simple one. When public opinion forms a strong consensus, this broad consensus is a good contra-indicator. The market is usually too bullish when prices have already risen (sharply) and too bearish when they have already fallen (sharply). 

### **If the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it is time**
