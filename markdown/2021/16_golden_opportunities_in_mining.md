In a typical bull market, **for every 1.0% increase in the gold price, the gold miners should appreciate 2-3% due to the leverage involved.** For 

example, if the gold price is USD 1,000/oz. and all-in costs are USD 950/oz., the net margin is USD 50/oz. If the gold price increases 10% to USD 1,100/oz., all-in costs are the same; the 10% increase in the gold price leads to a 20% increase in margins, from USD 50/oz. to USD 60/oz. While this is an extreme example, the same principle of margin expansion applies to smaller price increases as well. The opposite happened in 2020, with mining stocks underperforming the gold price.
