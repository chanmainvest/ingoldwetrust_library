## **_5) World Gold Mining index / Gold (currently 1.7x)_** 

Currently the ratio of the World Gold Mining index and gold is 1.7x and thus above the longterm median of 1.4x. A rise indicates that gold shares are outperforming gold. The chart shows that since the beginning of the bull market shares have not outperformed gold anymore. Gold mining shares have performed more or less in line with the gold price.
