# **d. When inflation strikes: Silver!** 

Analogous to the gold price we want to take a look at the inflation-adjusted silver price as well. The current price of silver doesn't even look particularly expensive in nominal terms. **Adjusted for CPI and Shadow Stats inflation rates, the price of silver is definitely at a historically extremely low level.**
