## Key Takeaways 

- Forecast confirmed, conservative price target reached. Our expectation of a “golden decade,” formulated in 2020, has materialized: In 2025, gold hit numerous new all-time highs in every fiat currency and achieved its strongest annual growth since 1979 at +65%. In January 2026, the price reached a new alltime high of USD 5,595. 

   - The next US dollar bear market may be just beginning. Since 1971, the DXY has experienced three pronounced bear markets of -35%, -51%, and -40%; the last 15 years of remarkable sideways movement, however, formed a multi-year cycle top. 

   - Consolidation yes, trend reversal no. Following the spectacular rise of recent months, the Sprott Gold Bullion Sentiment Indicator signaled euphoric levels; the current pause is therefore not surprising. 

- On a USD basis, the compound annual growth rate (CAGR) since 2000 has been 11%; on a EUR basis, 10.5%. Gold thus outperforms nearly every other asset class. 

- A controlled devaluation of the US dollar is the stated goal of the Trump administration – but from an economic policy perspective, this is a fallacy: A soft currency policy does not solve structural problems.

**So what are our expectations for the US dollar?** For us, one thing is clear: A controlled and significant devaluation of the US dollar is among Donald Trump’s key economic policy priorities. But it would be a grave mistake to artificially weaken the US dollar for the sake of reindustrialization, because currency devaluations do not solve structural problems – they generate inflation, make imports more expensive, and reduce the purchasing power of wages and savings at home and abroad, thereby severely weakening the very backbone of the US economy: domestic consumption. 

**_„We have currency problems…We have a big currency problem._** Donald Trump 

**Furthermore, a strong currency is a sign of international credibility.** Anyone who deliberately devalues the US dollar drives away the capital urgently needed to modernize infrastructure, expand production capacity, and secure technological leadership. The more sustainable path would lie in fiscal discipline, a streamlined bureaucracy, and targeted innovation support – tools that attract new industries and strengthen existing ones without squandering citizens’ wealth – including that of foreign creditors holding US dollar bonds – in a race to devalue. To put it bluntly: **If currency debasement were truly the path to prosperity, Zimbabwe would be the new Switzerland.**

## Conclusion 

- **_„We, in our sluggishness, do not realize that the dearness of everything is the result of the cheapness of money. For prices increase and decrease according to the condition of the money. An excessive quantity of money should be avoided._** Copernicus 

After a 64.4% increase in US dollars in 2025 – the strongest annual gain since 1979 – the gold price is in uncharted territory. Our long-standing thesis that reaching new alltime highs would herald a new trend phase has been confirmed. **A pronounced US dollar bear market could be a major positive catalyst in the coming years.** The DXY has formed a multi-year cycle top – similar to the patterns seen in the 1980s and early 2000s. In the coming quarters, the DXY is likely to move toward 90 or lower. 

Following the spectacular rise of recent months, the current consolidation in the gold price comes as no surprise – on the contrary, it was overdue. The Sprott Gold Bullion Sentiment Indicator recently showed a sentiment level that was already alarmingly close to the second standard deviation and thus in pronounced euphoria territory. **In the wake of the recent correction, however, this overheating has cooled noticeably – a classic washout that should lay a healthy foundation for the next upward wave.** 
