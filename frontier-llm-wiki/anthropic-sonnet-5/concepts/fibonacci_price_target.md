# Fibonacci Price Target

The Fibonacci Price Target is a technical-analysis tool used in the In Gold We Trust reports to project plausible future price levels for gold based on mathematical ratios derived from prior price corrections. By applying Fibonacci projections and extensions to recent market moves, the reports translate abstract chart patterns into concrete, quotable price objectives — giving readers a tangible benchmark for how much further the gold bull market could run.

## How the argument evolved

**2010** marked the first application of this method in the report's technical analysis section. Using the Fibonacci projection based on the last correction, the analysts calculated a specific extension level — "the Fibonacci projection level (261.8% of the last correction)" — which produced a target price of USD 1,600. This gave readers a precise, rules-based figure grounded in classical technical analysis rather than fundamental narrative alone.

By **2011**, the argument had matured and shifted upward. Rather than relying on a single projection ratio, the report now drew on multiple Fibonacci tools in combination, noting that "the most important Fibonacci projections and Fibonacci extensions" converged to suggest a medium-term technical price target of about USD 2,000. This represented both a methodological broadening — moving from one correction-based projection to a synthesis of several Fibonacci extensions — and a substantial upward revision of the implied target, reflecting the continued strength of the gold price trend between the two reports. The consistent use of Fibonacci-based targets across these two years established the technique as a recurring feature of the report's technical toolkit, used to lend quantitative precision to otherwise qualitative bullish narratives about gold's medium-term trajectory.
