# Midas Touch Gold Model

The Midas Touch Gold Model is the In Gold We Trust report's proprietary, multi-factor quantitative framework for analyzing the gold market, blending trend-following signals, contrarian positioning and sentiment data, cross-currency perspectives, and macro ratios into a single, holistic verdict. Rather than relying on any one indicator, it distills an extensive, disparate body of technical evidence into one compact, actionable conclusion, and it has served as the report's recurring barometer of gold's medium- to long-term trend across nearly a decade of publications.

## How the argument evolved

**2017** introduced the model as "a holistic approach to analyzing the gold market," explicitly built to integrate contrarian counter-cyclical signals — CoT positioning and sentiment data — alongside trend-following components tied to the gold price in terms of the Chinese yuan and Indian rupee, plus ratios and ETF flows. **2018** refined this architecture, describing the model's "goal... to rationally analyse the gold market" and detailing its building blocks: a monthly trend-following indicator as the foundation, followed by the "anticyclical (‘contrarian’)" CoT and sentiment signals, establishing the model's now-familiar structure of blending momentum with contrarian counterweights.

By **2019**, the model was being used for live directional calls, with the report offering "a comprehensive update on the current status" and concluding a bearish stance, advising "a neutral and patient stance until the typical summer low" as the model "moved back to a bearish conclusion." **2020** emphasized the model's synthesizing power — "it carefully examines many different perspectives on the gold market and brings them together conveniently in one table" — and used it to confirm a "medium to longer-term bullish outlook" via its first two components, marking a shift from the cautious 2019 reading toward renewed bullishness.

**2022** showed the model's responsiveness to changing conditions, flagging that it "switched into bearish mode on April 19," while reiterating its core value proposition of "summarizing an extensive analysis compactly and clearly in a table" to reach "a clear conclusion." **2024** highlighted the model's speed in catching inflection points: after a strong rally, it "last switched from bullish to neutral on April 15, 2024, thus recognizing the trend reversal on the gold market extremely quickly," even as underlying "buy signals" remained active on monthly and weekly charts, illustrating the model's layered, multi-timeframe nuance. **2026** continued this pattern of dynamic regime-switching, with the model spending "three months in bearish mode" before "finally" turning "neutral again on 7 May 2026," reaffirming its enduring role as the report's rational, holistic, and continuously updated gauge of gold's trend across market cycles.
