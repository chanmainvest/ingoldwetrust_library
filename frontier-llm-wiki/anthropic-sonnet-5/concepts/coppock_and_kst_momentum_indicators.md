# Coppock and KST Momentum Indicators

The Coppock curve and the Know Sure Thing (KST) indicator are long-term momentum tools used in the In Gold We Trust report's technical analysis to gauge the durability of gold's multi-year trend. Both are trend-confirming, rate-of-change-based oscillators whose sustained "buy" readings since 2015 have served as a recurring pillar supporting the report's structural bull case for gold, even amid short-term price wobbles or divergences.

## How the argument evolved

**2021** marked the baseline for this indicator pair in the report's technical chapters: both the Coppock curve and KST were shown to have flipped to "buy" territory at the end of 2015 and had remained there ever since. The report noted plainly that "since the end of 2015 the indicator has been on 'buy'," and that "the KST confirms the positive longterm setup" — establishing the two indicators as mutually reinforcing evidence of an intact long-term uptrend in gold.

By **2023**, the picture grew more nuanced. The Coppock indicator was still on a buy signal, but the report flagged that it "has formed a small divergence with the price," introducing a note of caution about weakening underlying momentum even as the headline signal stayed bullish. The KST, meanwhile, took on a more forward-looking role: it had "turned impulsively upward in the winter of 2022" and was framed as a possible harbinger of "an imminent breakout." This year's treatment thus split the two indicators' narrative functions — Coppock as a steady but slightly strained confirmation of the existing trend, and KST as a fresher, more dynamic signal pointing toward renewed upside.

The **2026** update shows continuity with 2023's framing rather than a fresh reversal. The Coppock indicator "remains at a buy signal, though it has formed a divergence from the price" — language that echoes the earlier divergence observation, suggesting this tension between price action and momentum had persisted or recurred over the intervening years. The KST is described as having "already turned impulsively upward in 2024 and continues to send a buy signal," extending the bullish impulse first spotted in the 2023 report into a multi-year confirmation. Taken together, the three chapters trace an arc: an initial clean, unified buy signal (2021), followed by a period where the two indicators diverge in behavior even as both remain technically bullish (2023), and finally a consolidation of that bullish momentum with the KST's earlier upturn proving durable while Coppock's divergence lingers as the one recurring caveat (2026). Across all three years, the indicators are consistently deployed as corroborating evidence for gold's structural bull market rather than as standalone trading signals.
