# Technical Analysis as Investment Tool

Technical analysis, in the framework of the In Gold We Trust reports, is treated not as a standalone or infallible discipline but as a practical complement to fundamental research — a means of pinpointing *when* and *where* to act on an investment thesis. Its significance lies in providing the "big picture" from a chart-based perspective, helping investors translate fundamental convictions about gold and related assets into concrete timing and positioning decisions.

## How the argument evolved

**2019** established the foundational stance on technical analysis that would carry through later editions: it is "certainly not an exact science, but rather a useful tool for determining the location and timing of investments." This framing positioned technical analysis modestly — acknowledging its imperfections while affirming its practical utility for investors trying to identify entry and exit points in the market.

**2021** reaffirmed this same core view almost verbatim, describing technical analysis as, "despite some weaknesses," a "useful tool for determining the location and timing of investments." What the 2021 treatment added was an explicit statement of methodology, emphasizing the importance of understanding "the 'big picture' not only from a fundamental but also from a technical perspective." This marked a subtle evolution: rather than presenting technical analysis in isolation, the report now framed it as one half of a dual lens — fundamental and technical — through which investment decisions should be assessed. The continuity between the two years underscores a consistent editorial philosophy: technical analysis is a supporting instrument, valuable for timing precision, but never a substitute for the deeper fundamental narrative that drives the reports' broader gold thesis.
