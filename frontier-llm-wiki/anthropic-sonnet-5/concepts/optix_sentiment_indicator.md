# Optix Sentiment Indicator

The Optix Index, sourced from Sentimentrader (later SentimenTrader), is the In Gold We Trust report's recurring contrarian sentiment gauge for gold, silver, and mining stocks. Built from sentiment surveys and positioning data, it rests on the premise that when public opinion forms a strong consensus, that consensus tends to be wrong — making low readings buy signals and high readings warnings of euphoria. Across seven years of technical analysis chapters, the Optix has served as a recurring checkpoint for gauging whether the gold complex is overheated, neutral, or oversold.

## How the argument evolved

**2018** introduced the Optix Index as one of the report's "favourite sentiment indicators," framed explicitly around the contrarian principle that "when public opinion forms a strong consensus, it is often wrong." With the Optix at 37, the reading was described as close to levels that "in the past indicated a good buying opportunity" — an early bullish signal for gold.

**2019** repeated the same contrarian framing almost verbatim, but the indicator had shifted to a "neutral level" at 46, offering neither a buy nor sell signal and reflecting a market without strong directional consensus.

By **2021**, sentiment had swung firmly toward optimism: the Optix stood at 70 for gold, described as "optimistic territory," while silver's reading of 71 was flagged as "close to the euphoria level" — a warning sign that both metals were becoming crowded trades after the pandemic-era rally.

**2022** saw sentiment cool considerably, with gold's Optix back to a "neutral territory" reading of 49, while silver at 39 was noted as "close to bearish territory," suggesting silver sentiment had swung further toward pessimism than gold's.

**2023** continued the neutral theme, with the monthly Optix at 61 "still in neutral territory" and silver at 55, now extending the indicator's coverage explicitly across "gold, silver, and mining stocks" as a triad of contrarian checks.

**2024** kept the narrative steady: gold's monthly Optix at 64 remained "still in neutral territory," and silver, now higher at 66, was "also still in neutral territory" — signaling that despite strong price action, the market had not tipped into the euphoria zone that would call for caution.

By **2026**, amid record gold prices, the report used the Optix to argue explicitly against overheating: "The Gold Optix stands at 59, the Silver Optix at 60, and the GDX Optix deep in bearish territory at 14." A new explicit threshold was introduced — "When the Optix Index rises above the red dotted line at 75 points, it's time to be more cautious" — sharpening the indicator's role as an actionable trigger rather than just a descriptive gauge. The mining-stock Optix reading of 14, "deep in bearish territory... even after the GDX has multiplied since 2022," underscored a striking divergence between price strength and sentiment, reinforcing the report's long-standing thesis that strong rallies need not be accompanied by crowd euphoria, and that the absence of extreme bullishness in the Optix left room for further upside.
