# Cup-Handle Chart Formation

The cup-handle formation is a classical technical-analysis pattern in which price traces a rounded "cup" followed by a shallower consolidation "handle," typically signaling that an eventual breakout will carry the market toward a measurable price target derived from the depth of the cup. In the *In Gold We Trust* report, this pattern has served as a recurring technical anchor for gold's long-term bullish case, offering a concrete price objective — around USD 2,700 — that analysts have tracked across multiple years as the formation slowly matures.

## How the argument evolved

The pattern first entered the report's technical analysis narrative in **2021**, where a long-term cup-handle formation dating back to August 2020 was identified as "particularly interesting" and flagged as a structure that "could now soon be resolved." At this stage, the formation was already advanced enough for analysts to calculate a price target, projecting that a successful resolution would carry gold toward "around USD 2,700."

By **2023**, the same cup-handle structure — now dated from 2020 more broadly — was still unresolved but remained a focal point of the technical outlook. The language echoed almost verbatim the earlier assessment, describing the formation as "particularly interesting" and again noting it "could now soon be resolved." Crucially, the price target itself had not changed: the report reaffirmed that the "price target of the formation remains at around USD 2,700," underscoring the durability of this technical projection even as the years passed without a definitive breakout. This continuity across two editions of the report highlights how the cup-handle pattern functioned less as a short-term trading signal and more as a long-running structural narrative — a slow-forming technical setup whose eventual resolution was consistently framed as a key marker for gold's next major upward move.
