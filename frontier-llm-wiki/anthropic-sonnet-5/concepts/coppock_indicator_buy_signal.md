# Coppock Indicator Buy Signal

The Coppock Indicator is a long-term momentum tool used in the In Gold We Trust reports' technical analysis chapters to identify major turning points in the gold market. Its significance lies in a single, recurring reference point: a long-term buy signal generated for gold at the end of 2015, which the report authors treat as a durable bullish anchor that continues to validate gold's multi-year uptrend even years later. The persistence of this citation across editions underscores its role as a slow-moving, high-conviction technical confirmation rather than a short-term trading trigger.

## How the argument evolved

**2018** marks the first appearance of the Coppock Indicator in this corpus, where the report notes that "the Coppock indicator generated a long-term buy signal at the end of 2015, and the sentiment still seems overwhelmingly sceptical." Here the signal is paired with a contrarian observation: despite the bullish technical trigger, market sentiment toward gold remained skeptical, framing the Coppock signal as an early, underappreciated indication that a long-term bottom had already formed.

By **2021**, the argument had not changed in substance but was reiterated as continued supporting evidence for a bullish technical outlook on gold. The report again states that "the Coppock indicator generated a longterm buy signal at the end of 2015," using the same historical reference point to reinforce the case that gold remained in a structurally positive long-term trend years after the original signal fired. Rather than searching for a fresh trigger, the analysis leans on the durability of the 2015 signal as an ongoing technical backdrop for the bull case.

**2022** repeats the identical framing almost verbatim — "The long-term Coppock indicator generated a buy signal for gold at the end of 2015" — showing that the report continues to treat this six-year-old signal as a still-relevant anchor for its technical narrative. Across all three editions, the core fact never changes: the signal date, the asset (gold), and its long-term nature remain constant. What evolves is less the indicator's message and more its role in the broader narrative — from an early contrarian flag in 2018 (bullish signal amid skeptical sentiment) to a standing pillar of the technical case in 2021 and 2022, cited without elaboration as established evidence supporting gold's secular uptrend. This pattern suggests the report authors view the Coppock buy signal not as a recurring event to be updated, but as a foundational, long-duration marker whose validity persists across market cycles.
