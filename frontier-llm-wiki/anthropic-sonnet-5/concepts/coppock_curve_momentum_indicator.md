# Coppock Curve Momentum Indicator

The Coppock Curve is a long-term momentum indicator used throughout the In Gold We Trust reports' technical analysis chapters to gauge the durability of gold's price trend. A buy signal is generated when the indicator turns upward from below the zero line, and its readings are treated as a slow-moving but highly reliable confirmation of major bullish or bearish turning points. Across successive years, the reports return to the Coppock Curve as a recurring anchor for their bullish long-term gold thesis, tracking its progression from initial buy signal through zero-line crossings and sustained upward drift.

## How the argument evolved

**2017** marked the indicator's introduction into the technical analysis chapters as "a very long term oriented and reliable momentum indicator." The authors noted that it "gave a buy signal in early 2015 and has gradually risen since then," and crucially, that year the Coppock "crossed the zero-line, which confirms the bullish setup" — a milestone interpreted as validating the broader case for gold.

**2018** revisited the indicator, this time dating its buy signal earlier, to the "beginning of 2014," after which it had "been gradually moving up ever since." Rather than a fresh zero-line crossing, the 2018 discussion emphasized a rebound: the Coppock curve "successfully rebounded from the zero line and thus confirmed the positive set-up," reinforcing continuity in the bullish narrative even as the precise dating of the original signal shifted slightly from the prior year's account.

**2019** refined the mechanics of the signal itself, clarifying that "a buy signal is given when the indicator turns up below the zero line." The report then anchored the ongoing signal to "the end of 2015," stating the indicator "has been on a buy signal since the end of 2015 and has been gradually moving upwards since then." This year's chapter also paired the Coppock Curve with a complementary momentum tool, noting the MACD "has also been on a buy since early 2016 and is slowly creeping up," situating the Coppock reading within a broader confirmation of persistent bullish momentum.

**2022**, after a gap in the notes covering the intervening years, the report returned to the same framework, again calling the Coppock Curve "a reliable momentum indicator" and reaffirming that "since the end of 2015, the indicator has been on 'buy.'" This edition added a new development: after presumably flattening or dipping, "since December 2021 it is now back on an upward trend" — extending the multi-year bullish narrative into the post-2020 period and demonstrating the indicator's role as a recurring, long-horizon confirmation tool that the reports lean on even amid shorter-term price volatility.

Across all four years, the throughline is consistent: the Coppock Curve is invoked as a dependable, slow-cycle validator of gold's long-term bull case, with each report updating readers on whether the buy signal first established in the mid-2010s remains intact.
