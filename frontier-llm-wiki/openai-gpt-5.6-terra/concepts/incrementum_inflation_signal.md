# Incrementum Inflation Signal

The Incrementum Inflation Signal is the report’s proprietary framework for assessing whether monetary inflation is reaching the real economy and for identifying changes in the inflation backdrop through financial-market indicators. Its significance lies in translating a broad monetary view of inflation into an actionable signal: one designed to detect rising tendencies, neutral conditions, and potentially the early stages of a sustained inflation cycle.

## How the argument evolved

**2014** The report introduced the conceptual foundation for the signal in its discussion of the interaction between inflation and deflation. Starting from the premise that “inflation is definitely a monetary phenomenon,” it sought “To measure how much monetary inflation actually reaches the real economy.” Rather than relying on a single measure, the proprietary approach used “a combination of various quantitative factors including the Gold-Silver Ratio.” The signal was therefore framed as a market-based attempt to distinguish monetary expansion itself from the extent to which that expansion is transmitted into real-economy inflation.

**2018** The framework had become explicitly identified as “the Incrementum Inflation Signal.” The report described its methodology in broad terms—“we monitor special indicators on the financial markets”—while using the resulting signal to characterize current inflation conditions. At that point, the assessment was directional rather than categorical: “our inflation signal indicates a rising inflation tendency at a signal strength of 50%.” This treatment extended the earlier emphasis on market-derived quantitative factors into a recurring tool for monitoring whether inflationary forces were strengthening.

**2025** The report emphasized the signal’s changing behavior under a more unsettled macroeconomic environment. It had “shown a significantly increased frequency of signal changes,” a development connected to “escalating trade tensions and the threat of a tariff conflict.” These conditions were described as “resulting in a return to a neutral signal,” illustrating that the framework can register not only rising inflation tendencies but also reversals or uncertainty. At the same time, the report retained the signal’s forward-looking role: its volatility and shifts may help identify a future sustained inflation cycle at an early stage, even when the immediate reading is neutral.
