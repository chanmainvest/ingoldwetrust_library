# Inflation and Gold Prices

Inflation is presented in the *In Gold We Trust* reports as both a central investment consideration and a major driver of gold-price dynamics. The core argument is that rising price inflation can create conditions supportive of a sustained gold-price advance, making gold and related investments relevant within inflation-sensitive portfolios.

## How the argument evolved

**2016** The report framed changing inflation dynamics as a potentially important catalyst for gold. It argued that rising price inflation could “tip the scales toward a sustained upturn in the gold price,” linking inflation not merely to short-term market moves but to the possibility of a more durable advance. This framing extended beyond bullion itself: “inflation-sensitive investments including gold and gold mining stocks will benefit.” Inflation was therefore treated as a macroeconomic development capable of improving the outlook for both gold and gold-mining equities.

**2020** The treatment became more explicit about inflation’s strategic importance. Rather than appearing chiefly as a prospective supportive condition for gold, inflation was described as “one of the central themes of our investment strategies.” The report also identified it directly as “one of the most important factors influencing the gold price: inflation.” This wording places inflation at the center of the analytical framework for gold, reinforcing the earlier view that inflationary developments can underpin gold’s appeal while emphasizing their broader significance for investment strategy.

Across these chapters, the argument develops from a conditional observation about rising price inflation supporting a sustained gold-price upturn into a more comprehensive investment thesis. Gold is positioned as an inflation-sensitive asset whose price outlook is materially shaped by inflation, while gold-mining stocks are included among the investments expected to benefit from such an environment.
