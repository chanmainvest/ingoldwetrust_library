# Coppock Curve Buy Signal

The Coppock Curve Buy Signal is the report’s long-term technical indication that gold’s underlying trend has turned or remains favorable. Its significance lies in its use as a framework for identifying major changes in direction rather than short-term market noise: when the curve rebounds, turns upward, or stays positively configured, the report treats this as confirmation of a constructive setup. Across the examined chapters, the signal is presented as persistent evidence of a long-running bullish technical condition.

## How the argument evolved

**2018** The report described the Coppock Curve as remaining in a positive configuration after it had rebounded from zero in early 2018. That rebound mattered because “this is seen as a buy signal,” framing the move not merely as an indicator fluctuation but as a technically meaningful change in momentum. The chapter also situated the signal within a longer history, stating that “The indicator switched to buy at the beginning of 2014.” By early 2018, the curve’s recovery from zero therefore “confirmed the positive set-up,” reinforcing the report’s view that the broader technical environment for gold remained supportive.

The 2018 treatment emphasized confirmation: the indicator had already shifted to buy years earlier, and its subsequent behavior was read as validating that stance. Rather than presenting the Coppock Curve as a standalone prediction tool, the discussion used its positive configuration and rebound to support a continuing bullish interpretation. The buy signal was thus associated with the endurance of a favorable trend as well as with the initial transition into one.

**2022** The later chapter retained the same long-term perspective while placing greater emphasis on the curve’s ability to identify turning points. It stated that “Since the end of 2015, the indicator has been on ‘buy’,” again characterizing the Coppock Curve as having maintained a durable positive signal. The report further observed that “since December 2021 it is now back on an upward trend,” adding renewed upward movement to the already-existing buy condition.

This presentation developed the earlier argument from confirmation of a positive setup toward a more explicit account of trend detection. The report noted that “you can reliably detect major trend changes,” explaining why the curve’s renewed ascent mattered within its technical-analysis framework. In this reading, the Coppock Curve’s value was not limited to labeling a market as bullish: its long-term buy signal and upward turn were treated as tools for recognizing major directional shifts and for assessing whether the broader positive trend remained intact.
