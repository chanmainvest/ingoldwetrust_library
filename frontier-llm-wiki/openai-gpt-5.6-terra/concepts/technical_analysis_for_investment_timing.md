# Technical Analysis for Investment Timing

Technical analysis is presented in the In Gold We Trust reports as a practical aid for deciding the location and timing of investments. Its significance lies not in replacing broader investment judgment, but in complementing fundamental assessment and, in the 2019 treatment, macroeconomic perspective. Across the two chapters, the reports consistently frame technical analysis as useful while avoiding any claim of precision or certainty.

## How the argument evolved

**2019** The report introduces technical analysis with an explicit qualification: “Technical analysis is certainly not an exact science.” This caveat establishes the intended role of the discipline within an investment process. Rather than offering a mechanical or definitive answer, it is described as “a useful tool for determining the location and timing of investments.” The formulation is important because it connects analysis of market behavior to two practical decisions: where an investment may be positioned and when it may be made.

The 2019 chapter also places technical analysis within a wider analytical framework. Investment decisions are not to be viewed only through charts or technical signals; they are considered “fundamentally and from a macro point of view” as well. Technical analysis therefore appears as an additional perspective alongside fundamental and macro evaluation. Its value is complementary: it can inform timing and positioning while broader fundamental and macro considerations remain part of the assessment.

**2021** The later chapter retains the central practical claim with nearly identical language, stating that “technical analysis is a useful tool for determining the location and timing of investments.” This continuity indicates that the report continues to regard technical analysis as relevant to investment implementation rather than as a separate substitute for investment analysis.

The 2021 framing emphasizes the relationship between technical and fundamental perspectives. Investments should be assessed “not only from a fundamental but also from a technical perspective.” Compared with the earlier chapter’s explicit inclusion of macro considerations and warning that technical analysis is not exact, the later wording is more concise and focused on the dual perspective of fundamentals and technicals. Yet the underlying argument remains stable: sound investment timing and location can benefit from technical analysis when it is used alongside, rather than instead of, fundamental assessment.
