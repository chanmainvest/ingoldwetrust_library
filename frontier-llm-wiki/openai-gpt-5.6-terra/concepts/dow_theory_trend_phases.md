# Dow Theory Trend Phases

Dow Theory Trend Phases divide a market trend into accumulation, public participation, and distribution. In the reports’ technical-analysis discussion, this framework serves as a way to describe the progression of trends and, later, to apply that progression specifically to gold’s market cycle. Its significance lies in giving the reports a consistent three-part vocabulary for interpreting how a trend develops.

## How the argument evolved

**2019** The report introduced the concept as part of Dow theory’s broader treatment of market trends. It stated that “Dow divided each trend into 3 different phases:” and identified this as “the subdivision of 3 trend phases.” The initial presentation framed the phases as a general market-trend structure: accumulation, public participation, and distribution. The emphasis on “1) Accumulation phase:” established accumulation as the first stage in this sequence, before the trend moves through public participation and ultimately distribution.

This treatment positioned the phases as components of a single trend rather than as separate or unrelated market conditions. By dividing each trend into three stages, the report presented Dow theory as a framework for tracing a market’s progression. The terminology also distinguished the early accumulation stage from the later stages in which broader participation and distribution occur.

**2020** The report retained the same Dow-theory structure but made its application more specific by using the three phases for gold’s market cycle. It introduced the framework again with the phrase “Here again are Dow’s three phases:” and explicitly listed “1) Accumulation phase:” followed by “2) Participation phase:”. In this iteration, the phases were not merely a general description of market trends; they were applied to the cycle of gold.

The shift from a general explanation in 2019 to a gold-market application in 2020 shows continuity in the report’s technical vocabulary. Accumulation, participation, and distribution remained the defining stages, while the later chapter used them to interpret gold’s cycle. The recurring presentation of the phases underscores their role as an organizing structure within the reports’ technical analysis of gold.
